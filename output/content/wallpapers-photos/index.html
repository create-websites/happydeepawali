Deepawali 2026 in short

Diwali is on Sunday 8 November 2026, with Lakshmi Puja from 5:54 to 7:50 PM IST in New Delhi. The five days run from Dhanteras (6 Nov) to Bhai Dooj (11 Nov), with Govardhan Puja on 10 November in India. The season opens with Navratri on 11 October, Dussehra on 20 October and Karwa Chauth on 29 October, and closes with Dev Deepawali in Varanasi on 24 November. It is the first Diwali since UNESCO added Deepavali to its intangible cultural heritage list in December 2025.

The five days of Diwali 2026

Each day has its own deity, story and ritual. Together they move from cleansing to wealth, light, gratitude and family.

Day 1
6 Nov

Dhanteras

Dhanvantari Trayodashi. Homes are cleaned, the first diyas lit, and new metal bought for luck.

  • Dhanvantari and Kuber puja in the evening
  • Yama Deepam: a four-wick lamp facing south
  • Buy gold, silver, brass or a new broom
Day 2
7 Nov

Choti Diwali

Naraka Chaturdashi, Krishna's victory over Narakasura. Roop Chaudas in Rajasthan, Kali Chaudas in Gujarat.

  • Abhyanga snan (oil bath) before sunrise
  • Rangoli at the door, diyas at dusk
  • Kali Chaudas protection rites in Gujarat
Day 3
8 Nov

Diwali · Lakshmi Puja

Kartik Amavasya, the darkest night lit brightest. Ram returns to Ayodhya; Lakshmi visits clean, lit homes.

  • Lakshmi-Ganesh puja 5:54–7:50 PM IST
  • Kuber and bahi-khata puja
  • Kali Puja in Bengal, Odisha, Assam
Day 4
10 Nov

Govardhan Puja

Kartik Shukla Pratipada. Krishna lifts Govardhan; Annakut, Bali Pratipada and Gujarati New Year (Bestu Varas).

  • Govardhan of cow dung, parikrama
  • Annakut: 56 dishes as bhog
  • Diwali Padwa for couples in Maharashtra
Day 5
11 Nov

Bhai Dooj

Yama Dwitiya. Sisters pray for brothers' long life, as Yamuna did for Yama.

  • Tilak in the afternoon (Aparahna)
  • Aarti, sweets and gifts
  • Bhai Phonta, Bhau Beej, Bhai Tika

In 2026 there is a gap day in India: Amavasya still covers sunrise on Monday 9 November, so Govardhan Puja moves to Tuesday 10 November. In the USA, UK and Canada, Govardhan Puja is on 9 November. In Tamil Nadu and much of the south, the Deepavali oil bath follows the Chaturdashi that covers the pre-dawn hours; check your local panchang.

Puja vidhi

Diwali Lakshmi Puja vidhi: samagri, step-by-step puja and mantras

Everything for the Diwali night puja at home, from what to buy to the last aarti, plus quick vidhis for the other festivals.

Lakshmi Puja is the heart of Diwali: the evening when the goddess of prosperity is welcomed into a clean, lit home, after first worshipping Ganesh. This is a complete home puja in the north Indian tradition, written so a first-timer can follow it and an experienced family can use it as a checklist. Allow about an hour, and start inside the muhurat for your city.

Lakshmi Puja vidhi, step by step

  1. Clean, decorate and bathe

    Lakshmi is said to enter clean, bright homes. Finish the cleaning by afternoon, hang a toran of mango leaves and marigolds, draw rangoli at the entrance with small Lakshmi footprints walking in, and bathe and wear clean, preferably new, clothes before dusk.

  2. Set up the altar

    Choose the north-east of the home or the north side of the room, so that you face east or north while sitting. Cover the chowki with a red cloth. Place Ganesh on the left and Lakshmi on the right as you face them, with Saraswati and Kuber beside them if your family worships them. Make a small heap of rice in front for the kalash.

    Family custom varies on placement; follow yours where it differs.

  3. Install the kalash

    Fill the kalash three-quarters with water and add a little Gangajal, a coin, a supari, a few grains of rice and a flower. Place five mango leaves at the mouth and set the coconut on top. Tie moli around its neck, mark a swastik in roli and place it on the rice heap to the right of the deity.

  4. Purify and light the lamp

    Sprinkle water on yourself, the samagri and the altar for pavitrikaran. Sip water three times (achaman). Light the large ghee diya, the akhand deep that will burn through the night, and place it to the right of the deity.

    ॐ अपवित्रः पवित्रो वा सर्वावस्थां गतोऽपि वा। यः स्मरेत् पुण्डरीकाक्षं स बाह्याभ्यन्तरः शुचिः॥Whether pure or impure, whoever remembers the lotus-eyed Lord is purified within and without.

  5. Take the sankalp

    Hold water, rice, a flower and a coin in your right palm. Say your name, your gotra if you know it, the place and the day (Kartik Amavasya, Sunday 8 November 2026) and your intention: Lakshmi-Ganesh puja for the well-being and prosperity of the family. Release it onto the ground or a plate.

  6. Worship Ganesh first

    Every puja begins with Ganesh, the remover of obstacles. Offer roli, rice, durva, a flower, and a laddoo or modak.

    वक्रतुण्ड महाकाय सूर्यकोटि समप्रभ। निर्विघ्नं कुरु मे देव सर्वकार्येषु सर्वदा॥O curved-trunked, mighty Lord, bright as a crore suns: keep all my work free of obstacles, always.

    ॐ गं गणपतये नमः

  7. Worship the kalash and the Navagraha

    Offer roli, rice and a flower to the kalash, inviting Varuna and the sacred rivers into it. Many families also offer rice to the nine planets at this point; it is optional.

  8. Invite Lakshmi and offer the sixteen upacharas

    Bring Lakshmi into the image with folded hands, then offer the sixteen services (shodashopachara). If time is short, the five essentials (panchopachara) are gandha, pushpa, dhoop, deep and naivedya.

    1. Avahaninvite the goddess
    2. Aasanoffer a seat
    3. Padyawater to wash the feet
    4. Arghyawater to the hands
    5. Achamanwater to sip
    6. Snanbath: panchamrit, then pure water
    7. Vastracloth or moli
    8. Abhushanornaments, sindoor
    9. Gandhachandan and roli
    10. Pushpaflowers, lotus, garland
    11. Dhoopincense
    12. Deepthe lamp
    13. Naivedyakheel, batashe, sweets, fruit
    14. Tamboolpaan, supari, cloves
    15. Dakshinacoins and money
    16. Aarti and pradakshinalamp, bell, circling

    ॐ श्रीं ह्रीं श्रीं कमले कमलालये प्रसीद प्रसीद श्रीं ह्रीं श्रीं ॐ महालक्ष्म्यै नमः॥Mahalakshmi mantra: O Kamala, dweller in the lotus, be pleased with us.

  9. Chant and pray

    Chant ॐ श्रीं महालक्ष्म्यै नमः 108 times on a lotus-seed or crystal mala, or recite the Shri Suktam or the Lakshmi Ashtottara (108 names). Offer kamal gatta and coriander seeds into a bowl as you chant.

    Families that cannot recite at length can simply sit quietly, offer flowers with each name, or play a recording.

  10. Kuber, Saraswati and bahi-khata puja

    Worship Kuber, keeper of wealth, with ॐ कुबेराय नमः, and the coins and cash placed before Lakshmi. Traders and families with account books draw a swastik and write शुभ लाभ on the first page of the new book, and worship the books, pens and ink as Saraswati. In Gujarat this is Chopda Pujan.

  11. Deep daan around the home

    Light the small diyas from the main lamp and place them at the threshold, in each room, in the kitchen, near the water source, by the tulsi, on the roof and at the gate. Keep one lamp facing each direction outside.

  12. Aarti

    Ring the bell, blow the conch and wave the aarti thali clockwise before Ganesh and then Lakshmi while singing the family aarti, usually ॐ जय लक्ष्मी माता and जय गणेश देवा. Offer the aarti flame to everyone present, then pushpanjali (flowers with folded hands) and a short prayer asking forgiveness for any mistakes.

  13. Prasad and the night

    Share kheel, batashe and sweets as prasad, starting with the youngest and eldest. Keep the akhand diya safely topped up through the night. Many families avoid sweeping the house out after the puja and keep the doors and a window open for Lakshmi.

  14. The next morning

    Sprinkle the kalash water through the home and pour the rest into a plant. Keep the worshipped coins with your savings and the kamal gatta in the cash box. Clay idols are kept until the next Diwali or immersed with respect.

How the puja differs across India

Kali Puja · Bengal, Odisha, Assam

  • Kali is worshipped at midnight (Nishita) on the same Amavasya.
  • Many homes also keep Lakshmi Puja in the evening.
  • Fourteen diyas (choddo prodip) are lit the night before.

Chopda Pujan · Gujarat

  • New account books are worshipped on Diwali night.
  • Bestu Varas, the Gujarati New Year, follows on 10 November.
  • Kali Chaudas rites on 7 November.

Lakshmi Pujan · Maharashtra

  • Lakshmi Pujan with a broom worshipped as Lakshmi.
  • Faral: chakli, karanji, shankarpali and ladoo.
  • Diwali Padwa and Bhau Beej follow.

Deepavali · Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra

  • The main day is Naraka Chaturdashi, with an oil bath before dawn.
  • New clothes and crackers in the morning.
  • Lakshmi Puja on Amavasya in many homes; Bali Padyami in Karnataka.

Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Start the puja inside the muhurat for your city.
  • Use ghee or sesame oil in the main lamp.
  • Keep the altar clean and well lit.
  • Worship Ganesh before Lakshmi.
  • Keep a bucket of water or sand near the diyas.

Avoid

  • Leaving a lit lamp near curtains, rangoli powder or children.
  • Broken or chipped idols on the altar.
  • Black clothes for the puja, by most traditions.
  • Arguments, meat and alcohol on the day, if your family observes this.
  • Rushing: a short, attentive puja is better than a long, distracted one.

Travel

Special places to experience Deepawali

Where the festival is at its brightest, from Ayodhya's ghats to the Dasara palace of Mysuru.

Ayodhya, Uttar Pradesh

Deepotsav · eve of Diwali

Lakhs of diyas on the Saryu ghats where Ram came home. The 2025 Deepotsav lit over 26 lakh lamps, a Guinness record.

Tip: Confirm the 2026 Deepotsav date with UP Tourism before booking.

Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh

Dev Deepawali · 24 Nov

Every ghat from Assi to Raj Ghat lit with lamps, the Ganga aarti, and boats on a river of light.

Tip: Book a boat weeks ahead; Dashashwamedh is busiest.

Amritsar, Punjab

Bandi Chhor Divas · 8 Nov

The Golden Temple lit and reflected in the sarovar, with fireworks over the complex.

Tip: Go early to find a place around the sarovar.

Jaipur, Rajasthan

Diwali week

The Pink City's bazaars, Johari and Bapu Bazaar and Hawa Mahal, lit in competition for the best decoration.

Tip: Walk the old city after 7 PM.

Delhi

Dhanteras to Diwali

Chandni Chowk and Sadar Bazaar for shopping, Connaught Place and Akshardham for lights.

Tip: Use the metro; the old city jams by evening.

Kolhapur, Maharashtra

Lakshmi Puja · 8 Nov

Mahalakshmi Temple, one of the Shakti Peethas, draws devotees for darshan on Diwali.

Tip: Expect long darshan queues on the day.

Quick answers

Deepawali 2026: questions people ask

When is Diwali in 2026?

The main night of Diwali, with Lakshmi Puja, is Sunday 8 November 2026. The five days run from Dhanteras on Friday 6 November to Bhai Dooj on Wednesday 11 November, and Dev Deepawali in Varanasi follows on Tuesday 24 November.

What is the Lakshmi Puja muhurat for Diwali 2026?

In New Delhi it is 5:54 PM to 7:50 PM IST on 8 November: the Vrishabha Kaal inside Pradosh Kaal (5:31 to 8:09 PM), per Drik Panchang. Mumbai is 6:27 to 8:27 PM, Dubai 5:58 to 7:56 PM, London 4:35 to 6:12 PM, New York 5:04 to 6:52 PM and Seattle 4:58 to 6:40 PM, each in local time.

When are Navratri, Dussehra and Karwa Chauth in 2026?

Sharad Navratri begins with Ghatasthapana on Sunday 11 October, Durga Ashtami is Monday 19 October in India, Dussehra (Vijayadashami) is Tuesday 20 October, and Karwa Chauth is Thursday 29 October in India and Wednesday 28 October in the USA and Canada.

Is Govardhan Puja on 9 or 10 November 2026?

In India most panchangs, including Drik Panchang for New Delhi, give Tuesday 10 November, when Pratipada is present at sunrise; the morning muhurat in New Delhi is 6:40 to 8:50 AM. Some temples keep the evening of 9 November. In the USA, Canada and the UK it is Monday 9 November.

Why do muhurat times change from city to city?

Each puja is tied to a local moment such as sunrise, sunset or the rising of a fixed lagna, while the tithi changes at the same instant worldwide. So the window has to be worked out for your own city; converting an Indian time to your time zone gives the wrong answer.

What do I need for Lakshmi Puja at home?

Lakshmi and Ganesh idols or a picture, a chowki with red cloth, a kalash with water, mango leaves and a coconut, roli, haldi, rice, flowers, incense, ghee diyas, panchamrit, sweets, kheel-batashe, fruits, paan, supari, coins and your account books or pens. The full checklist on this page saves your ticks on your device.

When is Dev Deepawali 2026?

Tuesday 24 November 2026, on Kartik Purnima, fifteen days after Diwali. The ghats of Varanasi are lined with lakhs of diyas in the evening. A few listings give 25 November, so confirm before booking a boat.

Which cities are best to experience Deepawali?

Ayodhya for Deepotsav on the eve of Diwali, Varanasi for Dev Deepawali, Amritsar for Bandi Chhor Divas at the Golden Temple, Jaipur for its lit bazaars, Kolkata for Kali Puja, Delhi for Chandni Chowk and Connaught Place, and Mysuru and Kullu for Dasara earlier in the season.
