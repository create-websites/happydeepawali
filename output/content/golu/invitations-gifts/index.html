What is thamboolam?

Thamboolam is the traditional gift given to guests, especially married women, as they leave a Golu. It usually includes betel leaves and areca nut, a banana or other fruit, turmeric and kumkum, often with sundal and a small return gift. Guests in turn visit, admire the dolls and often sing a song before they go.

Golu invitations and gifts: quick answers

Who should you invite to a Golu?

Families usually invite relatives, friends and neighbours to visit on one or more evenings of Navaratri. Traditionally women and children did most of the visiting, and today many households welcome whole families. Choose a number your space and preparations can handle.

How do you invite people for Golu?

Traditionally invitations were given in person, and many families still visit or call close relatives. Phone and WhatsApp messages are now common: share the dates, the visiting hours and your address, and send a short reminder closer to the day.

What should you take when visiting a Golu?

Nothing is required. Guests come to see the display, often sing a song, and receive sundal and thamboolam from the host. Some guests bring a small gift or sweets for the family, which is welcome but optional.

What is a good return gift for Golu?

Most hosts give thamboolam (betel leaves, fruit, turmeric and kumkum) together with a small useful item. Reusable, handmade and eco-friendly gifts are popular, and children often receive books, stationery or small toys.

Can you hold a Golu for family who live abroad?

Yes. Many families share their Golu over video calls, send photos or short videos of the steps, or host an online gathering so that relatives in other countries can take part.
