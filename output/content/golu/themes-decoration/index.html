What is a Golu theme?

Beyond the traditional steps of deities, saints and everyday life, many families build a theme each year: a set of famous temples, a village or river landscape, a story from the epics, or a children’s science scene. A theme usually fills one or more steps or a separate platform, while the traditional dolls and the kalash stay at the heart of the display.

Golu themes and decoration: quick answers

What are popular Golu theme ideas?

Popular themes include famous temples, scenes from the Ramayana or the Dashavatara, village and river landscapes, Indian heritage and crafts, nature and the environment, women of India, and science or learning themes for children.

Can you create a Golu theme without buying new dolls?

Yes. Rearrange the dolls you already have around a story or place, and build the theme with scenery, handmade props, labels and lighting. A new arrangement can feel as fresh as a new set of dolls.

How can you decorate a Golu on a budget?

Reuse sarees or cloth to cover the steps, decorate with flowers, rangoli and handmade paper or clay pieces, and borrow scenery materials from around the house. A few well-placed lights and a clear theme matter more than expensive decorations.

How do you make a Golu eco-friendly?

Choose clay, wood and natural decorations, use cloth and paper instead of plastic and thermocol, reuse decorations from year to year, and compost flowers and natural materials after Navaratri.

Is a modern or minimalist Golu acceptable?

Yes. Many families keep a smaller or more modern display, especially in apartments. Keeping the kalash, the deities and the daily puja at the centre preserves the spirit of Golu, and family custom guides the rest.
