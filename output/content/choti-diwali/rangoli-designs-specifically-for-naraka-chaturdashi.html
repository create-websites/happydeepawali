Rangoli Designs Specifically for Naraka Chaturdashi

Article 32 of 50 · 2 min read · Prashant Dhingra

Choti Diwali/Lamps & home preparation/Article 32

A Naraka Chaturdashi rangoli can surround a lamp, illuminate an entrance, or serve as a communal artistic activity. There is no single mandatory design that distinguishes it from Lakshmi Puja rangoli. Lotus shapes, dots, circles, and geometric edges commonly feature in festive home decorations.

Choose a design for the space

For a tight entrance, use a small circle surrounded by a ring of petals. A larger courtyard can feature a dot grid or a repeated border pattern. Ensure there is ample clear floor space so people can enter without walking over the design.

Build from a simple outline

Identify the center, define the outer edge, and progress from broad shapes to finer details. Using two or three colors often appears more harmonious than a busy range. Allow children to color within a contained area while an adult oversees the main outline.

Select materials thoughtfully

Rice flour, petals, and appropriate rangoli powders produce various effects. Take into account the floor surface, ease of cleaning, and who might come into contact with the materials. Refrain from using loose glitter near food or areas where it could disperse outside.

Add light without crowding the artwork

Position a lamp on an individual heat-resistant stand away from the walking path. Battery-powered lamps are effective for intricate setups. Capture a top-down photo of the completed arrangement before the evening gets crowded, and document the pattern to modify it for next year.
