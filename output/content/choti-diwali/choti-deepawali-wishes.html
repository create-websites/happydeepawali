A festival of light, many beautiful traditions

Choti Diwali, also known as Naraka Chaturdashi, is observed in different ways across India. It is known as Kali Chaudas in Gujarat and Bhoot Chaturdashi in Bengal, while many families in Goa mark the occasion with Narakasura effigies. In Tamil Nadu, Naraka Chaturdashi is closely associated with the main Deepavali celebrations.

Wherever and however you celebrate, may the warmth of every diya bring joy, good health, and prosperity.
