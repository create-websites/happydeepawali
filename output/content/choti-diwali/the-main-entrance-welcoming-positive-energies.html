The Main Entrance: Welcoming Positive Energies

The entrance establishes the atmosphere for a Choti Diwali home. A tidy doorway, simple decor, and an unobstructed path inside can help guests feel welcomed.

Article 40 of 50 · 2 min read · Prashant Dhingra

Choti Diwali/Lamps & home preparation/Article 40

The entrance establishes the atmosphere for a Choti Diwali home. A tidy doorway, simple decor, and an unobstructed path inside can help guests feel welcomed. Symbolic ideas of prosperity and positive energy can sit alongside the practical work of welcoming people comfortably.

Begin with access

Clear the path of shoes, deliveries, and loose cords. Ensure the door opens completely and that lighting is sufficient for people to notice any steps or floor level changes. Prepare a spot for older guests to rest and remove their shoes if this is a household tradition.

Choose a simple visual centre

A toran, a small rangoli, or a single decorated tray can offer sufficient detail. Footprint motifs are linked to Lakshmi’s welcome in certain households, but they are not mandatory for Choti Diwali designs. Use the symbolism that your family truly embraces.

Place lamps outside the walking line

Use a sturdy, heat-resistant base and ensure flames are kept away from hems, doormats, and dried flowers. Battery-operated lamps work well near high-traffic doorways. A striking entrance should never force people to pass too closely by a flame.

Complete the welcome personally

Welcome guests, guide them on where to leave their shoes, and offer them water. The feeling of a home relies on this simple gesture of hospitality just as much as on the décor showcased at the entrance.
