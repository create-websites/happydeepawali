Hanuman Puja on Choti Diwali

Article 8 of 50 · 2 min read · Prashant Dhingra

Choti Diwali/Stories & significance/Article 8

In certain North Indian families, Hanuman worship is part of the Diwali celebrations, particularly during the Chaturdashi observance. The emphasis lies on devotion, strength, service, and reassurance. This practice is regional or household-specific, not a mandatory ritual for all who observe Choti Diwali.

Prepare a calm worship space

Tidy the space surrounding an existing image or murti and place a small lamp, water, flowers, and a customary offering. A printed prayer book can assist everyone in participating. Ensure the arrangement is simple enough to remain easy to oversee and clean up afterward.

Offer according to established practice

Sindoor and oil are associated with Hanuman worship in some traditions. Ask the temple or follow your household's practice before applying anything to a murti. Pigments and oils can damage surfaces, and a temple may have specific rules about offerings.

Read with understanding

A brief devotional passage or a well-known segment of the Hanuman Chalisa can be paired with a discussion on service and bravery. Translating select lines for children might capture their interest better than hurrying through an extensive program they find hard to follow.

Carry devotion into action

Assist an elder, contribute to a household chore, or offer practical help to someone in need. Frame protection from harmful influences as a matter of faith, without asserting that any ritual invokes spirits or substitutes for regular care.
