Across India

Names and traditions of the festival

🪔

Naraka Chaturdashi

A common name across much of India; in some places the day is associated with early bathing rituals and lamps.

✨

Kali Chaudas

In Gujarat, Choti Diwali is widely known as Kali Chaudas.

🕯️

Bhoot Chaturdashi

In Bengali tradition, the observance is associated with lighting fourteen lamps.

🎇

Goa's Narkasur tradition

In Goa, celebrations often include the symbolic burning of Narkasur effigies.

Translation and image notes

Each greeting card uses the 400w image supplied in the image list. Its detail page displays the matching 1000w image. The first wish in each language was taken from the original supplied greetings page. The additional ten wishes per language are newly written editorial drafts; translations, especially Bodo, Kashmiri, Manipuri/Meitei and Santali, should be reviewed by fluent speakers before formal publication.
