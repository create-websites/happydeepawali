The Glowing Skin Diet for Roop Chaturdashi

Choti Diwali/Bathing & renewal/Article 18

A festive eating routine can help ensure a comfortable day without guaranteeing a radiant complexion. Choti Diwali usually occurs during a period of shopping, cooking, and late nights, so the best strategy is to have regular meals and allow sufficient time to enjoy them.

Build meals from familiar foods

A typical plate could consist of dal, vegetables, rice or roti, along with fruit or curd if these suit your usual diet. These suggestions are meant as meal ideas, not strict rules. Local favorites can be enjoyed alongside sweets without making the day feel overly restrictive.

Keep drinks straightforward

Provide drinking water for people as they cook and decorate. A well-known tea can be pleasant, but labeling a drink as detoxifying does not prove it eliminates toxins or improves the skin. Costly powders and elaborate cleansing routines are needless extras for the festival.

Make sweets part of hospitality

Serve a few pieces thoughtfully and allow guests to choose their portion. Offer an unsweetened drink and a savoury option as well. Avoid commenting on someone else's body or treating a festive dessert as a moral failure.

Plan for the busy hours

Prepare a simple meal ahead of evening worship. Those with medical conditions should maintain their regular diet and medication routine instead of switching to an online festive diet. Prioritizing care is more valuable than hoping for immediate glow.
