Fasting Rules and Saatvik Diets for Choti Diwali

Fasting customs on Choti Diwali differ among communities and households. Not everyone observes a strict fast, and many families mark the occasion with an oil bath, sweets, and…

Article 50 of 50 · 2 min read · Prashant Dhingra

Choti Diwali/Food, hosting & celebration/Article 50

Fasting traditions on Choti Diwali differ among communities and households. Not everyone observes a strict fast; many families mark the occasion with an oil bath, sweets, and festive dishes. The best place to begin is with the practice your own family follows.

Clarify the observance

Inquire if the household observes a fast, excludes certain ingredients, or opts for a vegetarian dish. The meaning of Saatvik varies: some families omit onion and garlic, while others follow a broader range of restrictions. Clarify this custom to guests instead of assuming they are familiar with it.

Offer a straightforward meal

Rice or roti accompanied by a common dal and a vegetable dish can create a simple meal where this is customary. Ensure there is a clearly marked choice for anyone avoiding a specific ingredient. It is unnecessary to cook an elaborate second meal just to make a dietary restriction appear special.

Keep personal needs visible

Individuals with medical needs requiring a specific eating or medication routine should maintain their plan. Children and other visitors should not be forced to fast. Engaging in religious practices can involve prayer, fellowship, and service without the challenge of dietary restrictions.

Share the meaning without judging

A family may connect restrained eating with attention or gratitude. That intention does not make someone else's meal less worthy. End the day with hospitality that allows people to state their needs comfortably and enjoy the festival together.
