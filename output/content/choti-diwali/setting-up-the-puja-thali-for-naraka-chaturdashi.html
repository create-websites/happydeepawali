Setting up the Puja Thali for Naraka Chaturdashi

A Naraka Chaturdashi puja thali collects all the items the household requires for the ritual itself. The goal is to have an orderly, accessible setup.

Article 38 of 50 · 2 min read · Prashant Dhingra

Choti Diwali/Lamps & home preparation/Article 38

A Naraka Chaturdashi puja thali collects all the items the household requires for the ritual itself. The goal is to have an orderly, accessible setup. An expensive silver platter or a large number of offerings is unnecessary unless a specific family tradition calls for it.

Start with the intended worship

Determine if the evening includes a typical household puja, Hanuman worship, a Yama-associated lamp, or a different tradition. These are not necessarily identical rituals. Request the worship leader to check the items prior to starting the preparations.

Assemble a modest checklist

A clean tray, water, flowers, an appropriate lamp, wicks, traditional oil, rice, and a common offering can fulfill many household requirements. Include kumkum or other items only if they are typically used. Keep a small cloth and a designated spot for packaging close at hand.

Arrange for easy handling

Place small containers in stable spots to prevent tipping, ensuring there is enough space to lift each item without touching a flame. Keep edible offerings apart from pigments and oils. Children may assist by arranging flowers and naming the items before the lamp is ignited.

Prepare for the end as well

Determine the distribution of prasad and the placement of cooled lamps. Neatly remove materials once worship concludes. An effective thali facilitates a smooth ritual from start to finish, avoiding turning the preparation into a shopping spree.
