Vastu Shastra for Choti Diwali Lighting

Choti Diwali/Lamps & home preparation/Article 31

Vastu-related explanations of Choti Diwali lighting frequently link directions, doorways, and energy flow. These are customary design principles, not assured promises of wealth or safety. An effective home layout can honor family choices while beginning with solid foundations and unobstructed pathways.

Draw the room before lighting

Highlight the entrance, puja area, and spots where people will sit or walk. Select one or two focal points instead of placing a flame in every corner. A thoughtfully arranged display allows for normal household activity.

Understand the directional custom

In certain families, a south-facing lamp might be part of a Yama-related offering. Lamps in other directions serve different purposes. Before enforcing a single directional guideline for the whole house, inquire about the specific ritual being conducted.

Adapt fourteen-lamp arrangements

The fourteen-lamp custom is particularly linked to Bengali Bhoot Chaturdashi and is not a mandatory Vastu practice for every Choti Diwali household. If your family observes this tradition, arrange the lamps together in visible spots instead of placing them in unsafe areas.

Let care guide the final placement

Utilize heatproof holders, keep fabrics at a safe distance, and opt for battery-operated lamps in hard-to-reach spots. A dark corner does not indicate that a home harbors negative energy. The arrangement can convey hospitality through careful placement, without making the layout a cause for concern.
