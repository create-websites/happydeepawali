How to Find Your Shraddha Date: A Simple Guide

Determining the right day is easy and depends on the lunar day, or Tithi, of an ancestor's death. This chart outlines the clear steps to follow.

Identify the Ancestor's Tithi

Identify the lunar day (e.g., Panchami, Dashami) of their passing.

Check for Special Cases

Was the death unnatural? (Refer to Chaturdashi). Did she pass away as a married woman before her husband? (Refer to Navami).

Match the Tithi

If no exceptions arise, locate the matching Tithi in the Pitru Paksha calendar and carry out Shraddha accordingly.

Unknown Tithi?

If the Tithi is unclear or the date is missed, observe the rituals for all ancestors on Sarva Pitru Amavasya.

The Spiritual Significance

Pitru Paksha serves as a holy link between generations, where Shraddha is performed as a heartfelt tribute to ancestors. This ritual is thought to bring peace to their souls while inviting blessings of health, prosperity, and harmony for the family. It is a moment to celebrate our heritage and recognize the foundation that influences our present.
