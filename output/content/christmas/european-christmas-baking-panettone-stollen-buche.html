European Christmas Baking: Panettone, Stollen and Bûche

These three famous Christmas desserts originate from various pastry customs, meaning mastering them requires understanding the unique behaviors of enriched dough, fruit bread, and rolled sponge.

Panettone: fermentation and structure

Panettone, a traditional Italian bread, is characterized by its tall stature, airy texture, and inclusion of candied citrus and raisins. The preparation of this bread often requires extensive fermentation and vigorous dough development.

Even with simplified recipes, home bakers must still focus on gluten strength, precise fermentation, and ensuring the tall loaf cools without sinking.

Stollen: dense, buttery and make-ahead

German stollen is typically dense and packed with dried fruit, nuts, spices, butter, and sugar, with a possible addition of marzipan.

Stollen, unlike panettone, can actually get better with time, making it a great option for holiday prep as it doesn't have to be baked and served on the same day.

Bûche de Noël: sponge and decoration

The traditional French yule log is a rolled sponge cake that is filled and frosted to look like a log. The key challenge lies in the cake's flexibility: it must be rolled while still soft enough to bend without breaking.

Decorations can vary from basic fork-textured chocolate frosting to intricate meringue mushrooms and fancy pastry-shop embellishments.

Choose one skill level at a time

For beginners in holiday baking, start with stollen or a simplified yule log before trying the more intricate panettone. Be sure to use a scale, thoroughly read the recipe, and factor in proofing or chilling time before getting started.

Each of the three items freezes or holds in a different way, so be sure to arrange them according to the recipe rather than assuming that every Christmas bake is most delicious when eaten fresh out of the oven.

Context note: Enriched doughs may require extra time to proof in a cold winter kitchen, so rely on dough cues, not just the time on the clock.

Frequently asked questions

Which of these is easiest for beginners?

A basic stollen or easy rolled cake is typically easier to make than a traditional panettone.

Can these be made ahead?

Yes, storage varies for different types of baked goods. Stollen is best when allowed to rest, while panettone and rolled cakes require unique freshness techniques.
