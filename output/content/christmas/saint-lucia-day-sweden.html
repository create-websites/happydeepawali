Saint Lucia Day in Sweden: Light, Song and Saffron

On December 13, Lucia celebrations in Sweden are known for combining light, music, and hospitality to create a distinctive winter tradition.

Why Lucia is associated with light

Saint Lucia, a Christian saint, has her feast day integrated into Scandinavian winter culture, particularly in Sweden where the focus is on light during the darkest time of the year. The Lucia figure is typically dressed in white and adorned with a crown of candles or electric lights.

Today's celebration combines Christian symbolism with local customs, involving schools, workplaces, choirs, and family gatherings, rather than adhering to a single, unaltered medieval ritual.

The procession and songs

Lucia processions commonly feature participants in white robes, star boys, and choirs performing well-known seasonal tunes. Various institutions such as schools, churches, workplaces, and public spaces may organize their own renditions of the event.

Electric candles are now commonly used for safety, particularly around children, although real candles may still be seen in closely monitored environments.

Lussebullar and the food tradition

Saffron buns, also called lussebullar or lussekatter, are a traditional Lucia treat, commonly enjoyed with coffee, tea, or other cozy beverages. Their vibrant yellow hue and unique spiral design are closely associated with the holiday.

Ginger biscuits and other treats baked in December might be enjoyed alongside the buns, but the saffron bread stands out as the main culinary highlight of the day.

How to celebrate respectfully

For a cozy home celebration, try baking saffron buns, playing Swedish Lucia music, setting a dimly lit ambiance with safe candles, and emphasizing themes of service, light, and community when children join in, rather than focusing on perfect costumes.

Frequently asked questions

When is Saint Lucia Day?

It is observed on December 13.

What are lussebullar?

They are saffron-flavored Swedish buns strongly associated with Lucia season.
