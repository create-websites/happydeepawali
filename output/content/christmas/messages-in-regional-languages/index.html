Four thoughtful ways to share Christmas greetings

Christmas is a Christian celebration of the birth of Jesus Christ and also has secular expressions. Customs vary among denominations, regions and families; send greetings only where welcome.

01

Wish people celebrating

Share a Christmas wish with friends, neighbours or colleagues who celebrate; ask rather than assume someone observes the holiday.

02

Respect church customs

Some families attend Christmas Eve or Christmas Day services; service times and practices differ among denominations and parishes.

03

Share care and kindness

Family gatherings, charitable giving, carols, festive food and companionship can each express the season in different ways.

04

Honour regional languages

Choose fluent-reviewed local-language wording when available; use our clearly labeled English or Hindi bridges otherwise.

CHRISTMAS · CULTURE & DATE

One Christmas Day; many local traditions.

Christmas commemorates the birth of Jesus Christ. Christian families and congregations across India may mark it through worship, prayer, carols, nativity scenes, family gatherings, and service to others. Public seasonal decorations do not mean universal religious observance.

Goa has well-known Christmas and Natal festivities. Kerala has multiple ancient Christian traditions. In Meghalaya, Mizoram and Nagaland, diverse Christian communities celebrate in locally distinctive ways; in Kolkata, Christmas also has a highly visible civic dimension. Regional names, denominations and practices differ.

2026 observance: Friday, 25 December 2026. Date verified with Time and Date. This website was prepared 31 October 2026; it does not promise local parish schedules.
