Rockefeller Center Christmas Tree: History and Design

The Rockefeller Center Christmas tree is a massive feat of engineering and a beloved public tradition, turning a single majestic spruce into a nationally renowned symbol of the holiday season.

How the tradition began

The tradition of the Rockefeller Center tree can be traced back to the early 1930s, when workers put up a Christmas tree at the construction site. This led to an official annual lighting ceremony that has since become a significant event in New York City.

As time passed, the tree transformed from a local decoration into a national holiday symbol due to broadcast coverage, skating-rink imagery, and tourism.

How a tree is selected

Choosing the perfect tree usually entails selecting a sizable Norway spruce from the New York area, considering factors like its appearance, health, ease of removal, and the challenges of transporting such a tall tree on public roads.

When making the final decision, it's important to consider more than just height. Symmetry, branch density, and safe movement are equally important factors to consider.

Transport, rigging and lights

Transporting and setting up a tree of this size necessitates the use of cranes, protective covering, careful planning of the route, and a team working in sync. After positioning it securely, workers then add ample lighting and place the star on top.

The engineering may go unnoticed by visitors, but it plays a crucial role in ensuring the display can be safely maintained in a busy urban plaza for extended periods.

Visiting without the stress

For a more peaceful experience, consider visiting early in the day or during the off-peak season. Be prepared for crowd management measures in place during busy evenings and opt for public transportation whenever feasible.

Instead of attempting to visit every Midtown attraction in one short trip, consider incorporating the tree with nearby windows, churches, Bryant Park, or Fifth Avenue.

Context note: Before visiting Rockefeller Center, make sure to check the official information for updates on lighting dates, viewing rules, and security arrangements that may change each season.

Frequently asked questions

When did the Rockefeller Center tree tradition begin?

It is commonly believed to have originated in the early 1930s, followed shortly by the establishment of the annual lighting tradition.

What kind of tree is usually used?

A large Norway spruce is the most common choice.
