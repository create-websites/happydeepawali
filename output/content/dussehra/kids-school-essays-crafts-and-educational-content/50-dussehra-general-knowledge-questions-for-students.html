50 Dussehra General Knowledge Questions for Students

Discover 50 Dussehra General Knowledge Questions for Students, including cultural background, regional variations, practical tips, FAQs, and related Dussehra topics.

50 Dussehra questions and answers

What is Dussehra?

Dussehra is an important Hindu festival linked to Vijayadashami. Various regions highlight Rama’s triumph over Ravana, Durga’s defeat of Mahishasura, royal Dasara ceremonies, educational rituals, or other regional traditions.

Is Dussehra the same as Vijayadashami?

Although the terms frequently refer to the same festival day, their usage varies by region and the focus of the rituals differs.

When is Dussehra celebrated?

It is celebrated on Dashami following Navratri as per the Hindu lunar calendar. The corresponding Gregorian date varies annually.

Why is Ravana burned?

In numerous North Indian customs, Ravana Dahan symbolizes Rama’s conquest over Ravana and is seen as the victory of dharma over evil.

Is Ravana Dahan practiced everywhere?

No. Many regions celebrate Vijayadashami without burning effigies.

What is Ramlila?

Ramlila is a theatrical tradition that reenacts stories from the Ramayana.

How is Durga connected with Vijayadashami?

Shakta traditions celebrate Durga’s triumph over Mahishasura, and Vijayadashami signifies the end of Durga Puja in numerous eastern Indian communities.

What is Ayudha Puja?

Ayudha Puja is a festival that pays tribute to tools, instruments, vehicles, and equipment, particularly in South Indian customs.

What is Vidyarambham?

Vidyarambham is a ritual that marks the beginning of a child's education, particularly observed during Vijayadashami in Kerala.

What are Apta leaves?

In certain Maharashtrian customs, Apta leaves are traditionally exchanged as a symbol of “gold” on Dussehra.

What is Simollanghan?

It is a Maharashtrian tradition linked to symbolically crossing a threshold on Vijayadashami.

What foods are eaten on Dussehra?

Cuisine differs across regions and families and may consist of festive sweets, savory treats, prasad, and celebratory dishes.

Can Dussehra be celebrated at home?

Certainly. Families can pray, read or talk about festival stories, share meals, honor tools or books, exchange greetings, and set personal goals.

Is Dussehra a public holiday everywhere?

Holiday status varies by country, state, or organization. Refer to the appropriate local calendar.

What should visitors wear?

Simple, comfortable attire is suitable for temples and community gatherings; adhere to the guidelines of the local venue.

Are Dussehra dates and puja times the same everywhere?

No. Since calendar computations and local time zones are important, use a location-specific panchang to determine ritual timings.

What is Mysuru Dasara?

This is Karnataka’s primary Dasara festival, renowned for its cultural events, palace lighting, and the Vijayadashami parade.

What is Kullu Dussehra?

It is a Himachal Pradesh festival recognized for the assembly of local deities and customs, differing from Ravana Dahan.

What is Bijoya Dashami?

It is the Bengali Vijayadashami custom signifying the end of Durga Puja, bidding farewell to the goddess, and sharing greetings.

What is the core message of Dussehra?

Many individuals view the festival as a symbol of moral bravery, rejuvenation, and the triumph of positive values over negative ones.

What does Dussehra mean?

A widely used name for the festival celebrated on or near Vijayadashami, featuring diverse regional customs.

What does Vijayadashami mean?

The “tenth day of victory,” observed on Dashami after Navratri.

What does Dasara mean?

A regional spelling and name commonly used in Karnataka and certain areas of South India.

What does Navratri mean?

A nine-night festival cycle that precedes Vijayadashami in many traditions.

What does Dashami Tithi mean?

The tenth day of the lunar month; the festival's date varies according to the Hindu calendar and regional panchang.

The epic narrative central to many Rama-focused Dussehra traditions.

What does Rama mean?

The prince of Ayodhya, celebrated for his triumph over Ravana in numerous Dussehra customs.

What does Sita mean?

A key character in the Ramayana whose kidnapping by Ravana ignites the epic’s central conflict.

What does Lakshmana mean?

Rama’s brother and companion during exile.

What does Hanuman mean?

The loyal companion of Rama who is instrumental in finding Sita and aiding Rama’s mission.

What does Ravana mean?

The ruler of Lanka, who was conquered by Rama, is portrayed with differing character interpretations across various literary and regional traditions.

What does Kumbhakarna mean?

Ravana’s brother and a major warrior in the Ramayana.

What does Meghnad / Indrajit mean?

Ravana’s warrior son, known as Indrajit in the Ramayana tradition.

What does Vibhishana mean?

Ravana’s brother who sides with Rama after opposing Ravana’s actions.

What does Ramlila mean?

Dramatic performance traditions that retell episodes of the Ramayana.

What does Ravana Dahan mean?

The ritual of burning Ravana effigies during certain Dussehra festivities in North India.

What does Durga mean?

The goddess whose triumph over Mahishasura is fundamental to Shakta Vijayadashami customs.

What does Mahishasura mean?

The buffalo demon vanquished by Durga in a key story from a major Shakta festival.

What does Shakti mean?

Divine feminine power; a key concept in goddess-centered traditions.

What does Durga Puja mean?

A significant festival dedicated to Goddess Durga, particularly celebrated in eastern India.

What does Bijoya Dashami mean?

Bengali term and customs linked to Vijayadashami and the end of Durga Puja.

What does Sindoor Khela mean?

A ceremony featuring vermilion observed in certain Bengali Durga Puja communities during Vijayadashami.

What does Visarjan mean?

Ritual immersion or farewell of a deity's idol, including Durga Visarjan.

What does Aparajita Puja mean?

A Vijayadashami worship tradition observed in some communities.

What does Shami mean?

A tree associated with Vijayadashami traditions in several regions.

What does Apta Leaves mean?

Leaves exchanged symbolically as “gold” in some Maharashtrian Dussehra customs.

What does Simollanghan mean?

A Maharashtrian Vijayadashami tradition involving the crossing of a boundary or symbolic border.

What does Ayudha Puja mean?

The reverence and veneration of tools, instruments, vehicles, or equipment, particularly prevalent in South India.

What does Shastra Puja mean?

Worship of weapons or implements, practiced in some traditions.

What does Saraswati Puja mean?

Veneration of the goddess of knowledge, commonly associated with books, instruments, and learning.

Cultural context and respectful participation

Dussehra is best understood as a collection of interconnected Vijayadashami customs rather than a uniform practice observed by every Hindu household. A traveler might witness a Ramayana-focused Ramlila performance in one city, a Durga Puja send-off in another, Ayudha Puja or Saraswati rituals in the South, Maharashtrian Shami and Apta observances in the West, or unique deity-centered celebrations in regions like Kullu and Bastar. These variations contribute to the festival’s cultural diversity and should not be seen as anomalies requiring a single interpretation.

When attending a celebration outside your own tradition, first observe and seek permission before taking part in any ritual. Adhere to the rules of temples and venues, refrain from interrupting prayers or processions to take photos, and refer to local customs by their proper names. In school, workplace, or public settings, avoid portraying Ravana Dahan, Sindoor Khela, Vidyarambham, or other regional practices as universally part of every Dussehra celebration.

Practical planning notes

  • When the precise Vijayadashami tithi or muhurat is important, use a panchang specific to the location.
  • For public events, confirm the latest organizer, location, entry guidelines, and schedule rather than depending on outdated information.
  • Arrange transport and accessibility in advance for major fairs, processions, and Ramlila venues.
  • Ensure children are kept away from fire, fireworks, crowded edges, and temporary electrical setups.
  • Choose reusable decorations and lower-waste serving options where practical.
  • For family gatherings, tailor the day to match the regional customs and the degree of observance your household truly practices.
