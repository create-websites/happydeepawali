Vijayadashami in Bengal: Sindoor Khela, Bijoya and Immersion

Discover the vibrant Vijayadashami customs of Bengal, from Sindoor Khela and Bijoya greetings to the heartfelt farewell of Maa Durga following the Durga Puja festivities.

Why this guide matters: In Bengal, Vijayadashami signifies both triumph and goodbye, as the departure of Maa Durga evokes a mix of emotions and the anticipation of her return the following year.

The final day of Durga Puja

Following days of worship and community festivities, Vijayadashami signifies the end of Durga Puja. Devotees get ready for the Goddess's immersion with thankfulness, sentiment, and a public parade.

The day combines sacred closure with social renewal.

Sindoor Khela

Sindoor Khela is a traditional practice where married women apply vermilion to the Goddess and each other in certain communities. Participation in this ritual is evolving, with many celebrations becoming more inclusive in their interpretation.

While its public image is one of joy, the tradition is actually part of a series of farewell ceremonies.

Bijoya Sammelani and greetings

Once the formal puja is over, individuals exchange Bijoya greetings, visit loved ones, share sweets, and strengthen social connections. Bijoya Sammelani gatherings may continue even after the immersion period ends.

The conclusion of a religious celebration marks the start of a time for coming together and repairing relationships.

Immersion and impermanence

The emotional intensity of immersing Durga images stems from the deliberate act of returning something beautiful and meticulously crafted back to the water. This ritual imparts the lesson of impermanence while maintaining continuity in anticipation of the next year's return.

Victory, departure and renewal all coexist in the same moment.

Common questions

What is Sindoor Khela?

In certain Bengali Durga Puja traditions, it is a custom to use vermilion on Vijayadashami.

What does Bijoya mean socially?

After Durga Puja, it is a time for exchanging greetings, visiting loved ones, indulging in sweets, and rekindling relationships

Why is immersion emotional?

As Maa Durga leaves, she carries with her the promise of returning in the next annual cycle.
