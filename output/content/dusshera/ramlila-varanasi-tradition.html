Ramlila of Varanasi: Open-Air Theatre and Living Tradition

Explore the ancient open-air Ramlila customs of Varanasi, their UNESCO-acknowledged historical significance, and the communal storytelling experience that captivates audiences.

Why this guide matters: Ramlila goes beyond just a stage performance, with entire neighborhoods and public spaces being transformed into part of the storytelling backdrop in longstanding traditions.

The Ramayana performed in public space

Ramlila brings the Ramayana to life over several nights, typically ending with Dussehra. In Varanasi, renowned outdoor performances utilize various locations like streets, grounds, and open spaces instead of a traditional theater.

The audience has the opportunity to physically engage with the action, transforming passive spectatorship into an immersive experience akin to a pilgrimage.

UNESCO-recognized heritage context

Ramlila is recognized by UNESCO as intangible cultural heritage, and it remains a vibrant performance tradition encompassing oral storytelling, acting, music, costumes, community involvement, and traditional practices.

The cultural significance is found in passing down from one generation to the next, rather than solely in the ultimate presentation.

Why place matters

As a scene plays out in a real public setting, the geography intertwines with the storytelling. Different locations can represent Ayodhya, a forest, or a battlefield, and moving between them adds a sense of physical dimension to the narrative.

Long-form Ramlila can create an immersive experience even without the use of modern theatrical technology, for this is one of the reasons.

Watching respectfully

Visitors should be prepared for a solemn and reverent atmosphere during performances. Please respect processions, adhere to photography guidelines, and keep in mind that for many participants, this event is both a religious worship and a theatrical experience.

To truly grasp it, one must watch more than just the climactic scenes.

Common questions

Is Ramlila only performed on Dussehra day?

Numerous traditions span over multiple days or weeks and culminate during Dussehra.

Why is the Varanasi tradition distinctive?

The city landscape becomes an integral part of the narrative through open-air, multi-location performances.

Is Ramlila theatre or worship?

Its functionality can vary depending on the community and participants involved.
