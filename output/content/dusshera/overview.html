Why Vijayadashami matters

One festival, many forms of victory

Dussehra signifies various victories and celebrations, including Rama defeating Ravana, Durga overpowering Mahishasura, Vidyarambham symbolizing the return to learning, Shastra and Ayudha Puja honoring tools, and regional festivities honoring local deities and heritage.

This hub categorizes 20 guides into four groups, allowing readers to navigate from myth and ritual to travel, reflection, and practical celebration.
