Why Apta Leaves Are Exchanged as “Gold” on Dussehra

Discover the significance of exchanging Apta leaves as a symbol of "gold" on Dussehra in Maharashtra and neighboring cultures, and uncover the stories and social meanings behind this tradition.

Why this guide matters: The tradition transforms a simple leaf into a ceremonial offering, turning wealth into a symbolic gesture that goes beyond just monetary value.

A leaf that becomes “sona”

During Dussehra in western India, individuals exchange Apta tree leaves as a symbol of prosperity, calling them "sona" and offering well wishes for success and goodwill.

The tradition is unforgettable as it allows for abundance to be within reach: a mere leaf can symbolize a valuable present.

Legends of generosity

Different renditions of the tale illustrate the tradition by showcasing acts of kindness and sharing wealth. While the details may vary, they all emphasize the same moral principle: true wealth is found in the circulation of prosperity, not in its accumulation.

This is the reason why exchanges frequently occur between family members, neighbors, coworkers, and friends.

A social ritual of reconciliation

Sending festival greetings can serve as a simple way to reconnect after time apart or disagreements. Giving a symbolic gift of gold can act as a small gesture of respect and goodwill.

The value is relational, not financial.

Celebrate without harming trees

By adhering to tradition, only take what is necessary and refrain from harming trees by stripping branches. In cases of limited plant access, communities can utilize fallen leaves, shared bundles, or symbolic reusable cards.

The symbolism embodied by the living tree should not be sacrificed in pursuit of prosperity.

Common questions

Why are Apta leaves called gold?

They are exchanged as symbolic sona, representing prosperity and goodwill.

Is this custom practiced across India?

It is commonly linked to Maharashtra and certain nearby cultural practices.

Can the tradition be made eco-friendly?

Yes, only use small amounts of fallen leaves when necessary and be mindful of protecting trees from harm.
