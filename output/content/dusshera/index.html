About the 2027 date

Standard Vijayadashami is marked on Saturday, October 9, 2027, in India-centric calendars, whereas Bengali Vijayadashami may follow a different calculation and occur on Sunday, October 10, 2027. Precise puja timings vary by location, and public event timings should be verified with the respective organizers.
