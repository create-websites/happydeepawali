Dussehra Quiz for Kids: Questions and Answers

Discover the Dussehra Quiz for Kids: Questions and Answers featuring cultural insights, regional variations, practical tips, FAQs, and related information about Dussehra.

20 Dussehra questions and answers

What is Dussehra?

Dussehra is an important Hindu festival linked to Vijayadashami. Various regions highlight Rama’s triumph over Ravana, Durga’s defeat of Mahishasura, royal Dasara ceremonies, educational rituals, or other regional traditions.

Is Dussehra the same as Vijayadashami?

The terms are frequently applied to the same festival day, though their regional usage and ritual focus vary.

When is Dussehra celebrated?

It is celebrated on Dashami following Navratri as per the Hindu lunar calendar. The corresponding Gregorian date varies annually.

Why is Ravana burned?

In numerous North Indian customs, Ravana Dahan symbolizes Rama’s conquest of Ravana and is seen as the victory of dharma over evil.

Is Ravana Dahan practiced everywhere?

No. Many regions celebrate Vijayadashami without burning effigies.

What is Ramlila?

Ramlila is a theatrical tradition that reenacts stories from the Ramayana.

How is Durga connected with Vijayadashami?

Shakta traditions celebrate Durga’s triumph over Mahishasura, and Vijayadashami signifies the end of Durga Puja in numerous eastern Indian communities.

What is Ayudha Puja?

Ayudha Puja is a celebration that venerates tools, instruments, vehicles, and equipment, particularly within South Indian customs.

What is Vidyarambham?

Vidyarambham is a ritual that marks the beginning of a child's education, traditionally linked to Vijayadashami in Kerala.

What are Apta leaves?

In certain Maharashtrian customs, Apta leaves are symbolically traded as “gold” during Dussehra.

What is Simollanghan?

It is a Maharashtrian tradition linked to the symbolic act of crossing a boundary on Vijayadashami.

What foods are eaten on Dussehra?

Cuisine differs across regions and families and may encompass festive sweets, savory treats, prasad, and celebratory dishes.

Can Dussehra be celebrated at home?

Certainly. Families can pray, read or talk about festival stories, share meals, honor tools or books, exchange good wishes, and set personal goals.

Is Dussehra a public holiday everywhere?

Holiday status varies by country, state, or organization. Refer to the appropriate local calendar.

What should visitors wear?

Simple, comfortable attire is suitable for temples and community gatherings; adhere to the guidelines of the local venue.

Are Dussehra dates and puja times the same everywhere?

No. Since calendar calculations and local time zones are important, use a location-specific panchang to determine the correct ritual timing.

What is Mysuru Dasara?

This is Karnataka’s principal Dasara festival, famous for its cultural events, palace lighting, and the Vijayadashami parade.

What is Kullu Dussehra?

It is a Himachal Pradesh festival recognized for the assembly of local deities and customs, differing from Ravana Dahan.

What is Bijoya Dashami?

It is the Bengali Vijayadashami custom that signifies the end of Durga Puja, bidding farewell to the goddess and exchanging greetings.

What is the core message of Dussehra?

Numerous individuals view the festival as a symbol of moral bravery, rejuvenation, and the triumph of positive values over negative ones.

Cultural context and respectful participation

Dussehra is better understood as a collection of interconnected Vijayadashami customs rather than a uniform practice observed by all Hindu families. A traveler might see a Ramayana-based Ramlila performance in one city, a Durga Puja send-off in another, Ayudha Puja or Saraswati rituals in the South, Maharashtrian Shami and Apta ceremonies in the West, or unique deity-focused celebrations in regions like Kullu and Bastar. These variations contribute to the festival’s cultural diversity and should not be viewed as anomalies requiring a single interpretation.

When attending a celebration outside your own tradition, first observe and seek permission before taking part in any ritual. Adhere to the rules of the temple or venue, refrain from interrupting prayers or processions for photos, and refer to local practices by their proper names. In school, workplace, or public settings, avoid portraying Ravana Dahan, Sindoor Khela, Vidyarambham, or any other regional custom as a universal part of every Dussehra celebration.

Practical planning notes

  • When the precise Vijayadashami tithi or muhurat is important, use a panchang specific to the location.
  • For public events, confirm the current organizer, location, admission policies, and schedule rather than depending on outdated information.
  • Arrange transport and accessibility well in advance for major fairs, processions, and Ramlila venues.
  • Ensure children are kept away from fire, fireworks, crowded edges, and temporary electrical setups.
  • Choose reusable decorations and lower-waste serving options where practical.
  • For family gatherings, tailor the day according to the regional customs and the degree of observance your household truly practices.
