The complete Govardhan Puja collection

Pick a subject or search all 30 guides

Inspired by the story of Krishna lifting Govardhan Hill, this collection moves from sacred places in Braj to the Annakut thali and the details of celebrating at home. The travel guidance uses official district and temple references where available, and the recipes are adaptable home versions rather than fixed religious rules.

2026 planning note: Govardhan Puja in the Braj / Delhi region is listed for Tuesday, 10 November 2026. Local muhurat and temple programmes vary by location.
