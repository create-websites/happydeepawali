About these Shraddha messages

The collection brings together short messages for remembering ancestors and departed family members during Shraddha and Pitru Paksha. Some messages are general, while others are written for parents, grandparents, relatives, first Shraddh observances and Sarva Pitru Amavasya.

Choose wording that fits your relationship and family tradition. You can share a message as written or personalize names and relationships before sending it to relatives and friends.

Remember. Offer. Pray. Pitru Paksha, the sacred homage to our ancestors.
