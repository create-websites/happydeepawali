Leftover Easter Eggs: Beyond Deviled Eggs

Transform safely stored hard-boiled eggs into salads, savory pastries, grain bowls, and quick breakfasts.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🌼 EasterCulinary & Baking Trends

Start with safe leftovers

Ensure that only eggs that have been properly handled and refrigerated are used. Eggs that have been left outside for extended periods, stored in warm environments, or hidden in questionable locations should not be automatically considered for consumption.

Make a flexible egg salad base

Cut eggs into small pieces and mix with a dollop of mayo or yogurt, mustard, herbs, and crunchy veggies. Enjoy in sandwiches, lettuce wraps, wraps, or on toast instead of preparing a big batch that can get monotonous.

Add eggs to savory meals

Quartered eggs are a great addition to grain bowls, composed salads, curries, and potato dishes, while chopped eggs can enhance hand pies, stuffed pastries, or breakfast casseroles.

Use the yolks creatively

Using extra yolks in sauces and dressings and chopping whites into salads can make meals more versatile and reduce waste. Planning multiple meals with leftovers in mind can keep things interesting.

Frequently asked questions

Can I eat eggs used in an outdoor Easter hunt?

As long as they are stored properly and kept clean and intact, they should be safe to use.

What is an easy alternative to deviled eggs?

Egg salad, grain bowls, and savory breakfast dishes are convenient options for using multiple eggs in one meal.
