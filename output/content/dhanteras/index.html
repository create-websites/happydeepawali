Helpful context

Before you celebrate

When is Dhanteras in 2026?

Friday, November 6 in Delhi. Puja and purchase windows depend on local calendrical calculations.

Is Ayurveda Day on Dhanteras?

Not officially. Since 2025, India celebrates National Ayurveda Day on September 23 every year.

Is buying gold compulsory?

No. An affordable symbolic item, shared prasad, prayer or charity can honor the celebration.

Do customs vary?

Yes. Lamp placement, mantras, food and shopping beliefs differ significantly between families and regions.
