Deepawali 2026 on the ghats and in the temples

Where India lights the most lamps, from Ayodhya on the eve of Diwali to Varanasi on 24 November.

Deepawali (Diwali) 2026 falls on Sunday 8 November 2026. Lakshmi Puja muhurat in New Delhi is 5:54 PM to 7:50 PM IST. The five days run from Dhanteras on 6 November to Bhai Dooj on 11 November, with Govardhan Puja on 10 November, and Dev Deepawali in Varanasi follows on 24 November. It is the first Diwali since UNESCO inscribed Deepavali as intangible cultural heritage in December 2025.

A route through the festival

Deepotsav, eve of Diwali (expected Sat 7 Nov)

Ayodhya, Uttar Pradesh

Diwali begins here in the story: the lamps lit by the people of Ayodhya to welcome Ram, Sita and Lakshman home after fourteen years. Deepotsav on the ghats of the Saryu now holds the Guinness World Record for the most lamps lit at once. At the ninth Deepotsav on 19 October 2025, Ayodhya lit 26,17,215 diyas across 56 ghats of the Saryu, a count verified by Guinness World Records, while 2,128 Vedacharyas performed a maha aarti together and about 1,100 drones drew scenes from the Ramayana in the sky. Arrive at Ram ki Paidi by mid-afternoon if you want a place to stand.

Diwali and Bandi Chhor Divas, Sun 8 Nov

Golden Temple, Amritsar

Sikhs mark Bandi Chhor Divas on Diwali, remembering Guru Hargobind's release from Gwalior fort. The Harmandir Sahib and its sarovar are lit with lamps and lights, and the evening fireworks over the water draw enormous crowds.

Diwali week

Akshardham, Delhi

The carved sandstone complex on the Yamuna is lit for the festival and is an easy evening visit from anywhere in Delhi. Pair it with the market lights of Connaught Place and Chandni Chowk.

Lakshmi Puja, Sun 8 Nov

Mahalakshmi Temple, Kolhapur

One of the oldest shrines of the goddess, and a natural place for darshan on the night she is worshipped across India. Expect long queues on the day itself.

Through the five days

Siddhivinayak, Mumbai, and Shirdi

Every Diwali puja begins with Ganesh, so many Mumbai families visit Siddhivinayak during the festival. Shirdi, a few hours away, is busy all week with pilgrims lighting lamps at Sai Baba's samadhi.

Tue 24 Nov, Kartik Purnima

Varanasi: Dev Deepawali

Fifteen days after Diwali, Kashi lights every ghat for the gods' own Diwali, celebrating Shiva's victory over Tripurasura. Watch the Maha Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh and take a bajra or boat along the river for the long view from Assi to Raj Ghat. A few listings give 25 November; confirm locally.

Travelling for Diwali

Book early. Trains and flights into Ayodhya and Varanasi fill weeks ahead, and hotels near the ghats often need a minimum stay around the big nights.

Go on foot near the river. Roads to the ghats close to traffic on festival evenings; plan to walk the last stretch.

Dress for the puja, not the selfie. Cotton and silk are safer than loose synthetics around open diyas, and comfortable footwear matters on wet steps.

Keep the family puja. If you travel, carry a small diya and Lakshmi–Ganesh image so you can do the puja in your room during your city's muhurat.

The first Diwali as world heritage. On 10 December 2025 UNESCO inscribed Deepavali on its Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity, at the committee's twentieth session held at the Red Fort in New Delhi. It is India's sixteenth element on the list, recorded under social practices, rituals and festive events.

Buy from the ghats. Clay diyas, flowers and prasad from local sellers support the people who make these nights possible.

Deepawali 2026: questions people ask

When is Diwali in 2026?

The main day of Diwali, the night of Lakshmi Puja, is Sunday 8 November 2026, on Kartik Amavasya. The festival opens with Dhanteras on Friday 6 November and closes with Bhai Dooj on Wednesday 11 November.

What is the Lakshmi Puja muhurat for Diwali 2026?

Drik Panchang gives 5:54 PM to 7:50 PM IST for New Delhi on 8 November, the Vrishabha Kaal inside Pradosh Kaal (5:31 PM to 8:09 PM). Jaipur is 6:03 to 7:59 PM, Chennai 6:07 to 8:09 PM and Pune 6:24 to 8:24 PM. Check a panchang for your own city.

Is Diwali 2026 on 8 or 9 November?

8 November. Amavasya also touches the sunrise of 9 November, but Lakshmi Puja is done in the evening, and the Amavasya that covers the evening puja window falls on the 8th.

When is Govardhan Puja in 2026?

Drik Panchang lists Tuesday 10 November for most Indian cities, because Pratipada only reaches sunrise on the 10th. Some calendars, some cities and most of the western hemisphere keep it on Monday 9 November.

When is Bhai Dooj in 2026?

Wednesday 11 November 2026, on Kartik Shukla Dwitiya.

When is Dev Deepawali 2026 in Varanasi?

Tuesday 24 November 2026, on Kartik Purnima, fifteen days after Diwali. A few listings give 25 November, so confirm locally before booking a boat.

Is Diwali a UNESCO heritage?

Yes. Deepavali was inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity on 10 December 2025, making Diwali 2026 the first since that recognition.

Is Diwali on the same day in the USA and India in 2026?

Yes, the Lakshmi Puja night is Sunday 8 November in both, though the muhurat follows local sunset. The days around it can differ, so use a panchang set to your city.
