Light up Deepawali 2026

Lakshmi Puja is on Sunday 8 November. Here is how to make the house, the doorstep and the street look their best by then.

In short: Deepawali (Diwali) 2026 falls on Sunday 8 November 2026. Lakshmi Puja muhurat in New Delhi is 5:54 PM to 7:50 PM IST. The five days run from Dhanteras on 6 November to Bhai Dooj on 11 November, with Govardhan Puja on 10 November, and Dev Deepawali in Varanasi follows on 24 November. It is the first Diwali since UNESCO inscribed Deepavali as intangible cultural heritage in December 2025.

The cities that set the standard

Every October the markets go first. Delhi's Connaught Place wraps its white colonnades in light, Chandni Chowk hangs strings across lanes barely wide enough for a rickshaw, and Jaipur lights the pink city around the Hawa Mahal and the Raja Park market. Walk them in the evenings of the last week before Diwali, when the decorations are complete and the shops are open late.

Connaught Place, Delhi

Go after dark on a weekday; the inner circle is the most photographed.

Chandni Chowk, Old Delhi

Wholesale lights, diyas and puja goods, plus the best sweet shops in the city.

Hawa Mahal, Jaipur

The façade glows over a street full of decorated markets.

Raja Park, Jaipur

A neighbourhood market that dresses up for the festival.

A rangoli for the door

Rangoli at the entrance welcomes Lakshmi on the night of the 8th. Draw it on the evening of Choti Diwali so it is ready, then refresh the edges before the puja.

  1. Sweep and dampen the spot; colours hold better on a slightly damp floor.
  2. Mark a centre point and a light chalk grid of dots, or draw a circle with a string.
  3. Outline in white, then fill the centre first and work outwards so you don't smudge it.
  4. Finish with marigold petals and a ring of diyas at the outer edge.

Your decorating plan

Clean the house, the office and the car

Cleaning before Diwali clears out the old year: it is a way of removing negativity and making room for Lakshmi. Do the deep work in the last week of October so the days just before 6 November are free for shopping and decorating.

Decorate with diyas, rangoli and flowers

Diyas on the windowsill and balcony, rangoli at the door and marigold toran over it are the classic three. Mix clay diyas with LED strings or electronic diyas where open flame is risky, and buy handmade, eco-friendly pieces from local potters and artisans.

Buy local, and share

Choosing local potters, florists, sweet-makers and craftspeople keeps the money in your own neighbourhood. Set aside a share of the festival budget for people who need it: sweets, clothes or diyas for families who cannot buy them.

Plan the outfits

Sarees, kurta-pyjamas and lehengas in bright, traditional colours are the Diwali uniform. For the puja and around diyas, cotton and silk are safer than loose synthetics.

Gifts for family

Think about the person, not the price: a puja set for parents, a small personalised piece of jewellery for a sibling, a warm blanket for grandparents, or something handcrafted and a box of local delicacies. The thought behind it is what is remembered.

Gifts for employees and clients

Corporate Diwali gifting is a thank-you. Hampers of mithai, dry fruits and artisanal snacks remain the favourite; branded stationery, a good pen, useful tech, eco-friendly products, wellness kits, home décor, vouchers and engraved mementos all work. Small businesses can add festive offers or limited editions for customers.

Deepawali 2026: questions people ask

When is Diwali in 2026?

The main day of Diwali, the night of Lakshmi Puja, is Sunday 8 November 2026, on Kartik Amavasya. The festival opens with Dhanteras on Friday 6 November and closes with Bhai Dooj on Wednesday 11 November.

What is the Lakshmi Puja muhurat for Diwali 2026?

Drik Panchang gives 5:54 PM to 7:50 PM IST for New Delhi on 8 November, the Vrishabha Kaal inside Pradosh Kaal (5:31 PM to 8:09 PM). Jaipur is 6:03 to 7:59 PM, Chennai 6:07 to 8:09 PM and Pune 6:24 to 8:24 PM. Check a panchang for your own city.

Is Diwali 2026 on 8 or 9 November?

8 November. Amavasya also touches the sunrise of 9 November, but Lakshmi Puja is done in the evening, and the Amavasya that covers the evening puja window falls on the 8th.

When is Govardhan Puja in 2026?

Drik Panchang lists Tuesday 10 November for most Indian cities, because Pratipada only reaches sunrise on the 10th. Some calendars, some cities and most of the western hemisphere keep it on Monday 9 November.

When is Bhai Dooj in 2026?

Wednesday 11 November 2026, on Kartik Shukla Dwitiya.

When is Dev Deepawali 2026 in Varanasi?

Tuesday 24 November 2026, on Kartik Purnima, fifteen days after Diwali. A few listings give 25 November, so confirm locally before booking a boat.

Is Diwali a UNESCO heritage?

Yes. Deepavali was inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity on 10 December 2025, making Diwali 2026 the first since that recognition.

Is Diwali on the same day in the USA and India in 2026?

Yes, the Lakshmi Puja night is Sunday 8 November in both, though the muhurat follows local sunset. The days around it can differ, so use a panchang set to your city.
