How Karwa Chauth is kept

  1. Sargi before sunriseThe mother-in-law traditionally sends sargi: fruit, sweets, dry fruit, mathri and a little sevaiyan. It is eaten before dawn, after which the fast is nirjala, without food or water.
  2. ShringarWomen dress in bridal red or festive colours, with mehendi, bangles, sindoor and the full solah shringar.
  3. Evening puja and kathaIn the puja muhurat women gather with their thalis, worship Chauth Mata (Gauri), Shiva, Ganesh and Kartikeya, pass the thalis around the circle seven times and hear the vrat katha.
  4. Baya and karwaThe karwa, a spouted clay or metal pot filled with water, is exchanged, and the baya of sweets and gifts goes to the mother-in-law.
  5. MoonriseWhen the moon rises, look at it through a sieve, offer arghya of water from the karwa, then look at your husband through the same sieve.
  6. Break the fastThe husband offers the first sip of water and a sweet, and the family eats together.

What goes on the puja thali

  • Essential

  • Essential

  • Essential

  • Offering

    Sweets and mathri

    For the offering and the baya.

  • Puja

    Chauth Mata picture

    Or a Gauri image drawn on the wall or a calendar print, for the katha.

The vrat katha in brief

The best-known katha is of Veeravati, the only sister of seven brothers. Keeping her first Karwa Chauth fast at her parents' home, she grew faint before moonrise. Unable to bear it, her brothers lit a fire behind a tree and held up a sieve so that it looked like the rising moon. She broke her fast, and news came that her husband had died.

Grief-stricken, she kept faith through the year, and on the next Karwa Chauth kept the fast fully until the real moon rose. Her devotion moved the goddess, and her husband was restored to life. The story is why the fast is broken only after the real moon is seen, never at a guessed time.

Seeing the moon

  • Before moonrise

    Check the direction

    The moon rises in the east-northeast in India and the northeast in North America and Europe, low on the horizon at first.

  • At moonrise

    Give it a few minutes

    The calculated time is for a flat horizon. Over buildings or trees, first sight usually comes 5 to 15 minutes later.

  • Cloudy evening

    If clouds stay

    Many families offer arghya facing the direction of moonrise after the calculated time; others wait for a glimpse. Follow your family elders.

How the moonrise is calculated

  1. Moon positionA lunar theory accurate to about an arcminute gives the Moon's position and distance every few minutes for each city.
  2. MoonriseMoonrise is when the Moon's centre reaches the true horizon as seen from the surface, the convention published panchangs use. Times are rounded to the nearest minute.
  3. The dateKarwa Chauth is kept on the day Chaturthi (1:06 AM to 10:09 PM IST on 29 October) is current at moonrise. Where Chaturthi ends before moonrise, as in London and the Gulf, it is the day Chaturthi covers sunrise.
  4. Puja and fastThe puja muhurat runs from sunset for a tenth of the night; the fast runs from sunrise to moonrise; Brahma muhurta is the two night muhurtas before sunrise.

Karwa Chauth 2026: questions people ask

When is Karwa Chauth in 2026?

Thursday 29 October 2026 in India, on Kartik Krishna Chaturthi (Chaturthi runs from 1:06 AM to 10:09 PM IST). In the USA and Canada it is Wednesday 28 October, because there Chaturthi is current at moonrise on the evening of the 28th.

Karwa Chauth 2026 par chand kab niklega? (What time is moonrise?)

In New Delhi the moon rises at 8:17 PM IST on 29 October. Mumbai is 9:00 PM, Kolkata 7:47 PM, Chandigarh 8:12 PM, Jaipur 8:28 PM and Lucknow 8:06 PM. Pick your city in the finder for the exact time.

Why is Karwa Chauth on 28 October in the USA and Canada?

Chaturthi begins at 3:36 PM Eastern on 28 October and ends at 12:39 PM on 29 October. The moon rises in New York at 7:35 PM on the 28th, inside Chaturthi, so that is the Karwa Chauth evening there. By the next evening Chaturthi is over.

What is the Karwa Chauth 2026 puja muhurat?

In New Delhi Drik Panchang gives 5:38 PM to 6:56 PM, starting at sunset. Mumbai is 6:07 PM – 7:22 PM, New York 5:57 PM – 7:18 PM on 28 October and London 4:39 PM – 6:04 PM, local time.

What is the fasting time for Karwa Chauth 2026?

The nirjala fast runs from sunrise to moonrise: 6:31 AM to 8:17 PM in New Delhi, about 13 h 46 min. Sargi is eaten before sunrise.

When should sargi be eaten?

Before sunrise, traditionally in Brahma muhurta: about 4:48 AM – 5:39 AM in New Delhi on 29 October. The finder shows the window for your city.

Which direction should I look for the moon?

Low in the east-northeast in India and the Gulf, and in the northeast in North America and the UK. The Moon is a bright waning gibbous, about 84% lit, and hills or buildings can delay the first sighting by several minutes.

What if clouds hide the moon?

Many families wait a little longer, and if the moon still cannot be seen they offer arghya facing the direction of moonrise after the calculated time, or follow the custom of their family elders. Follow your own family's practice.

Is Karwa Chauth the same day as Sankashti Chaturthi?

Yes. Karwa Chauth falls on the Krishna Chaturthi of Kartik, which is also a Sankashti Chaturthi, so Ganesh is worshipped and the fast for both ends at moonrise.

How accurate are these moonrise times?

The calculator matches Drik Panchang's published moonrise to the minute for New Delhi, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, New York, Toronto and London. Moonrise is for a flat horizon; seeing the moon over buildings or trees takes a few minutes longer.
