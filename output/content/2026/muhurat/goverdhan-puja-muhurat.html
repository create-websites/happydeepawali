Govardhan Puja 2026 in short

In short

Govardhan Puja, or Annakut, falls on Kartik Shukla Pratipada, which runs from 12:31 PM IST on 9 November to 2:00 PM IST on 10 November 2026. In India it is kept on Tuesday 10 November in the morning: the Pratahkala muhurat in New Delhi is 6:40 AM – 8:50 AM (Drik Panchang), Mumbai 6:43 AM – 8:59 AM. Some panchangs and temples keep the evening of Monday 9 November instead (New Delhi 3:20 PM – 5:31 PM). In North America and the UK, Pratipada covers all of Monday 9 November: Chicago 6:33 AM – 8:33 AM and 2:35 PM – 4:35 PM, London 7:08 AM – 8:58 AM.

9 or 10 November?

The answer depends on where you are, and in India on which panchang your family or temple follows.

  • India, Gulf, Southeast Asia

    Tuesday 10 November, morning

    Pratipada is present at sunrise only on the 10th and ends at 2:00 PM IST, so the puja is done in the Pratahkala window (New Delhi 6:40 AM – 8:50 AM).

  • Also kept in parts of India

    Pratipada begins at 12:31 PM IST on the 9th, so some panchangs and temples keep the Sayankala that evening (New Delhi 3:20 PM – 5:31 PM).

  • USA, Canada, UK

    Monday 9 November, all day

    Pratipada covers the whole day: Chicago 6:33 AM – 8:33 AM or 2:35 PM – 4:35 PM; London 7:08 AM – 8:58 AM.

Annakut: the mountain of food

Annakut means a mountain of food. Kitchens cook dozens of dishes, traditionally 56 (chhappan bhog): sweets, rice, dal, kadhi, vegetables, puris, pickles and more. They are heaped into a hill before Krishna as Giriraj, the lord of Govardhan, and shared as prasad.

The festival remembers how Krishna persuaded the people of Braj to worship Govardhan hill, which fed their cattle, rather than Indra. When Indra sent seven days of storm, Krishna lifted the hill on his little finger and sheltered everyone beneath it.

Temples hold Annakut darshan through the day, with especially large displays at Govardhan and Mathura in Braj, at Nathdwara, and at ISKCON and BAPS mandirs worldwide.

How to do Govardhan Puja at home

  1. Make GovardhanIn the courtyard or on a clean floor, shape a small Govardhan hill from cow dung or clay, with figures of Krishna, cows and cowherds, and decorate it with flowers.
  2. Puja in the muhuratIn the Pratahkala window, offer roli, rice, flowers, water, milk, curd and sweets, and light a lamp and incense.
  3. Offer the AnnakutPlace the dishes before Govardhan and Krishna; even a few homemade dishes make a family Annakut.
  4. ParikramaWalk around the Govardhan seven times, sprinkling water or barley, while singing Krishna's names.
  5. Cows and prasadFeed and honour cows if you can, then share the Annakut with family, neighbours and those in need.

How the muhurat is calculated

  1. PratipadaKartik Shukla Pratipada runs from 12:31 PM IST on 9 November to 2:00 PM IST on 10 November, the same instants everywhere, converted to local time.
  2. The dayThe day on which Pratipada is present at sunrise. Where Pratipada ends before the evening of that day, as in India, the previous evening is shown as the alternative some panchangs follow.
  3. Five-part dayDaylight is divided into five equal parts. Pratahkala is the first and Sayankala the last, each kept only while Pratipada lasts.
  4. Sunrise and sunsetCalculated for each city with refraction, rounded to the nearest minute.

Govardhan Puja 2026: questions people ask

When is Govardhan Puja in 2026?

In India, Tuesday 10 November 2026, the morning after the Diwali Amavasya ends. Pratipada runs from 12:31 PM IST on 9 November to 2:00 PM IST on 10 November, so the puja is done in the morning of the 10th while Pratipada lasts. In the USA, Canada and the UK it is Monday 9 November.

Is Govardhan Puja on 9 or 10 November 2026?

Most Indian panchangs, including Drik Panchang for New Delhi, give Tuesday 10 November, when Pratipada is present at sunrise. Because Pratipada begins on the afternoon of the 9th, some panchangs and temples keep the evening of Monday 9 November. Follow your local temple or family panchang; the finder shows both windows for your city.

What is the Govardhan Puja muhurat in 2026?

In New Delhi the Pratahkala muhurat is 6:40 AM – 8:50 AM on 10 November (Drik Panchang: 6:40 to 8:50 AM). Mumbai is 6:43 AM – 8:59 AM. In Chicago it is 6:33 AM – 8:33 AM or 2:35 PM – 4:35 PM on 9 November.

What are Pratahkala and Sayankala?

The day from sunrise to sunset is divided into five equal parts. Pratahkala is the first part and Sayankala the last; Govardhan Puja is done in either, as long as Pratipada is current.

Why is there a gap day between Diwali and Govardhan Puja in 2026?

Diwali's Amavasya continues past sunrise on Monday 9 November in India, so the 9th still counts as Amavasya, and Pratipada is first present at sunrise on the 10th.

What is Annakut?

Annakut means a mountain of food. Families and temples cook dozens of dishes, often 56 (chhappan bhog), arrange them in a hill before Krishna as Giriraj, and share them as prasad after the puja.

Is Govardhan Puja the same as Bali Pratipada and Gujarati New Year?

They fall on the same Pratipada. Maharashtra and Karnataka keep Bali Pratipada (Diwali Padwa), and Gujarat celebrates Bestu Varas, the New Year, on 10 November in 2026.
