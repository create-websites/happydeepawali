The meaning

What is Bhai Dooj?

Bhai Dooj is a Hindu festival celebrating the bond between brothers and sisters, associated with Kartika Shukla Dwitiya in the Diwali season. Sisters commonly bless brothers with tilak, aarti and food, while families express care through gifts and time together.

Many regional names

One bond, many traditions

People also call the festival Bhai Phota, Bhau Beej, Bhai Bij, Bhai Tika or Yama Dwitiya. Practices vary by region and family, so use these guides for inspiration while honoring your own customs.
