Halwa, Ladoo and Diwali Faral Classics

Move into home-style Diwali classics that reward patient roasting, careful moisture control and crisp pastry technique, including halwas, ladoos, gujiya, mohanthal and peda.

  • Kalakand in 15 Minutes: A Quick Condensed Milk Recipe

    A modern shortcut to the traditional milk cake using paneer and condensed milk for last-minute prep.

  • Moong Dal Halwa: The Labor of Love

    Tips for soaking, grinding, and patiently roasting moong dal in ghee to achieve the perfect festive halwa.

  • Gajar Ka Halwa: Transitioning into Winter Sweets

    Traditional slow-cooked carrot pudding using seasonal red carrots, milk, and generous amounts of nuts.

  • Mysore Pak: The South Indian Ghee Delicacy

    Mastering the tricky timeline of pouring hot ghee into roasted besan to create the porous, melt-in-the-mouth texture.

  • Coconut Ladoo (Nariyal Ladoo): 3-Ingredient Quick Fix

    A fast and delicious recipe using desiccated coconut and condensed milk, perfect for beginner cooks.

  • Traditional Panjiri and Atta Ladoo

    Nutrient-dense whole wheat sweets packed with ghee, edible gum (gond), and dry fruits for winter strength.

  • Gujiya and Karanji: The Stuffed Fried Pastries of Diwali

    How to make the flaky outer crust and prepare traditional khoya, coconut, and dry fruit fillings.

  • Mohanthal: The Rich Gujarati Besan Fudge

    A step-by-step guide to this traditional sweet made with sweetened gram flour, ghee, and cardamom.

  • Shankarpali (Shakkarpara): Sweet Crispy Bites

    How to make the diamond-shaped, mildly sweet fried biscuits that are a staple in Maharashtrian Diwali faral.

  • Mathura Peda: The Classic Caramelized Milk Sweet

    How to roast khoya until deeply browned to achieve the authentic flavor of the famous temple town.
