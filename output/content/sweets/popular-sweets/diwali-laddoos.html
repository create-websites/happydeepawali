12 Types of Diwali Laddoos

Laddoos are the easiest Diwali sweet to make and share: besan, motichoor and boondi ladoos are the classics, rava and coconut the quick ones.

By Prashant Dhingra

See all 12 sweets Keeping guide

In short

Laddoos are the easiest Diwali sweet to make and share: besan, motichoor and boondi ladoos are the classics, rava and coconut the quick ones. Atta, dry fruit, gond and til-gud ladoos are the nourishing winter versions, and peanut, rajgira and makhana ladoos suit fasting or lighter plates.

About laddoos

Almost every Indian region has its own laddoo, and most can be shaped from a warm mixture with greased palms in minutes. Ladoos made with ghee and roasted flour keep for weeks; those with fresh coconut or khoya need refrigeration.

The 12 sweets

What each one is, what it is made of, and how long it keeps.

  1. 01 of 12

    Besan Ladoo

    LadooKeeps 2+ weeks

    Gram flour roasted slowly in ghee until golden and nutty, mixed with powdered sugar.

    Made with: Gram flour, ghee, powdered sugar, cardamom

  2. 02 of 12

    Motichoor Ladoo

    LadooKeeps about a week

    Made from very fine boondi soaked in syrup; soft and moist, often orange and studded with melon seeds.

    Made with: Fine gram-flour boondi, sugar syrup, melon seeds

  3. 03 of 12

    Boondi Ladoo

    LadooKeeps about a week

    Larger, crisper boondi bound with syrup; the temple prasad laddoo.

    Made with: Gram-flour boondi, sugar syrup, cardamom

  4. 04 of 12

    Rava / Suji Ladoo

    LadooKeeps about a week

    Roasted semolina with coconut and sugar; quick to make.

    Made with: Semolina, coconut, sugar, ghee

  5. 05 of 12

    Coconut / Nariyal Ladoo

    LadooRefrigerate, 2 to 4 days

    Coconut cooked with condensed milk or sugar and rolled in desiccated coconut.

    Made with: Coconut, condensed milk or sugar

  6. 06 of 12

    Atta Ladoo

    LadooKeeps 2+ weeks

    Whole wheat flour roasted in ghee with jaggery; a wholesome winter laddoo.

    Made with: Whole wheat flour, ghee, jaggery or sugar, nuts

  7. 07 of 12

    Dry Fruit Ladoo

    LadooKeeps 2+ weeks

    Dates and nuts pressed together with no added sugar.

    Made with: Dates, figs, almonds, cashews, pistachios

  8. 08 of 12

    Gond Ladoo

    LadooKeeps 2+ weeks

    Made with fried edible gum, wheat flour and ghee; traditionally given in winter and to new mothers for strength.

    Made with: Edible gum, wheat flour, ghee, jaggery, nuts

  9. 09 of 12

    Til-Gud Ladoo

    LadooKeeps 2+ weeks

    Roasted sesame bound with jaggery syrup; also the Makar Sankranti sweet.

    Made with: Sesame seeds, jaggery

  10. 10 of 12

    Peanut Ladoo

    LadooKeeps 2+ weeks

    Roasted peanuts with jaggery; crunchy, cheap and quick.

    Made with: Roasted peanuts, jaggery

  11. 11 of 12

    Rajgira Ladoo

    LadooKeeps 2+ weeks

    Puffed amaranth bound with jaggery; light and fasting-friendly.

    Made with: Puffed amaranth, jaggery

  12. 12 of 12

    Makhana Ladoo

    LadooKeeps about a week

    Roasted, crushed fox nuts with ghee and jaggery; a lighter, modern laddoo.

    Made with: Fox nuts, ghee, jaggery or sugar, nuts

Frequently asked questions

What are the most popular Diwali laddoos?

Laddoos are the easiest Diwali sweet to make and share: besan, motichoor and boondi ladoos are the classics, rava and coconut the quick ones. Atta, dry fruit, gond and til-gud ladoos are the nourishing winter versions, and peanut, rajgira and makhana ladoos suit fasting or lighter plates.

Which Diwali laddoos keep longest for gifting?

Besan Ladoo, Atta Ladoo, Dry Fruit Ladoo, Gond Ladoo, Til-Gud Ladoo, Peanut Ladoo and Rajgira Ladoo keep for two weeks or more in an airtight container, so they can be made first and are the safest to gift or courier.

Which Diwali laddoos need to be made last or refrigerated?

Coconut / Nariyal Ladoo are milk-, chhena-, curd- or fresh-coconut based or best served warm. Make them a day or two ahead at most, refrigerate, and serve within a few days.

Which other states make similar sweets?

Sesame and jaggery: Gajak (Rajasthan), Tilkut (Bihar).
