12 Types of Diwali Burfi

Burfi is a fudge-like sweet set in a tray and cut into squares or diamonds, which makes it the backbone of Diwali gift boxes.

By Prashant Dhingra

See all 12 sweets Keeping guide

In short

Burfi is a fudge-like sweet set in a tray and cut into squares or diamonds, which makes it the backbone of Diwali gift boxes. Kaju katli, mawa, besan and coconut burfi are the classics. Pista, badam, kesari, rose and mango add flavor and color, chocolate and tri-color are the modern favorites, and anjeer burfi is the no-added-sugar option.

About burfi

Most burfis start from a base of khoya (mawa), nuts, gram flour or coconut cooked with sugar until the mixture leaves the sides of the pan. Nut-based burfis such as kaju katli and anjeer burfi keep best for gifting; khoya burfis need refrigeration after a few days.

Frequently asked questions

What are the most popular Diwali burfi?

Burfi is a fudge-like sweet set in a tray and cut into squares or diamonds, which makes it the backbone of Diwali gift boxes. Kaju katli, mawa, besan and coconut burfi are the classics. Pista, badam, kesari, rose and mango add flavor and color, chocolate and tri-color are the modern favorites, and anjeer burfi is the no-added-sugar option.

Which Diwali burfi keep longest for gifting?

Kaju Katli, Badam Burfi, Anjeer Burfi and Besan Burfi keep for two weeks or more in an airtight container, so they can be made first and are the safest to gift or courier.

Which Diwali burfi need to be made last or refrigerated?

Mango Burfi, Rose Burfi, Kesari Burfi, Mawa Burfi and Tri-Color Burfi are milk-, chhena-, curd- or fresh-coconut based or best served warm. Make them a day or two ahead at most, refrigerate, and serve within a few days.

Which other states make similar sweets?

Gram flour and ghee fudges: Mysore Pak (Karnataka), Mysore Pak (Tamil Nadu), Mohanthal (Gujarat), Mohanthal (Rajasthan), Magaj (Gujarat), Besan Chakki (Rajasthan).
