Men’s Gudi Padwa Fashion: Kurta and Pheta

Achieve a stylish Maharashtrian festive ensemble with a kurta, pajama or dhoti, Nehru jacket, and the classic pheta while ensuring comfort.

Gudi Padwa Hub
Why this guide matters

Gudi Padwa comes alive with vibrant festivities through stylish clothing and home décor that are both festive and easy to achieve.

Start with the occasion

Consider the duration and activity level of the day before choosing the appropriate attire for a home puja, neighborhood procession, cultural program, or formal community event.

A well-fitted kurta made from breathable fabric can exude more elegance than a heavily embroidered piece that quickly becomes uncomfortable.

Kurta colors for the festival

The visual palette of Gudi Padwa harmonizes beautifully with saffron, cream, white, green, maroon, and gold-toned shades. Solid colors complement rangoli, marigolds, and the vibrant Gudi in photographs.

If the pheta is the focal point, opt for a simpler kurta to avoid a clash in the overall outfit.

Pajama, churidar or dhoti

Most wearers find a straight pajama or churidar to be a simple choice. Opting for a dhoti-style bottom can add a more traditional touch, but it is important to ensure it is tied securely and practiced beforehand, especially for longer events.

Pre-sewn dhoti pants are a convenient option for younger attendees and cultural gatherings.

The pheta as the focal point

The Pheta, a traditional Maharashtrian turban, has the ability to elevate a basic kurta into a unique festive ensemble. While saffron is a popular choice for public events and processions, various colors and regional tying techniques are also utilized.

When in doubt about tying a tie, seek help from a knowledgeable family member or opt for a pre-tied option to ensure a secure and polished look.

Add a jacket only when needed

During evening events or formal photographs, a Nehru jacket can add structure to a plain kurta. However, in hot weather, an extra layer may not be needed.

Comfort is essential in choosing festive attire. Opt for breathable fabrics and comfortable footwear for walking.

Coordinate without becoming identical

Families frequently plan color schemes for photos. Instead of matching perfectly, a more effective strategy is to opt for a cohesive palette: saffron with cream, green paired with gold, or maroon complemented by beige.

This maintains a unified look while still allowing individuals to showcase their own personal style.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

What is a pheta?

A pheta is a Maharashtrian turban traditionally worn for festive, ceremonial, and cultural events.

Does a Gudi Padwa outfit require a dhoti?

A kurta paired with pajama, churidar, or trousers can create a festive look, with the dhoti being a traditional choice.

Can I use a pre-tied pheta?

Yes, it can be practical if you lack someone with experience in tying the traditional style.
