GUIDE 21 OF 50 · Modern & Luxury Ideas

Smart Home Devices to Illuminate Their Diwali

Gifting tech that fits the festival: smart LED lighting strips, voice-controlled speakers for mantras, and smart plugs for outdoor lights.

Based on slide 22 of the Diwali Gift Guide · Expanded editorial guide

Read the guide ↓Browse all guides

Smart lighting and connected devices can extend Diwali decorating beyond a single night, provided they work in the recipient's home.

At a glance: Gifting tech that fits the festival: smart LED lighting strips, voice-controlled speakers for mantras, and smart plugs for outdoor lights.

What to consider before you choose

Check phone ecosystem, Wi-Fi availability, power voltage and whether the user wants voice control or simple manual operation. A smart plug may be less useful than a straightforward lamp for some people.

A strong choice starts with the person receiving it: their interests, household routine, cultural preferences, delivery circumstances and your comfortable spending limit. Use the source slide as inspiration, then personalize the final decision rather than treating any idea as mandatory.

How to put the gift together

Prefer reputable electrical products with applicable safety certifications. Set up the device before gifting when appropriate and provide straightforward instructions for turning features on and off.

Before committing, compare at least two suitable options for quality, practicality and total cost. Check how the gift will arrive and whether a simple handwritten greeting would make it more meaningful.

Details and mistakes worth avoiding

Do not assume an indoor-rated smart plug is safe outdoors. Subscription needs, privacy settings and troubleshooting burden can turn a clever present into an inconvenience.

Keep the recipient's convenience in mind. A gift that needs difficult storage, complex setup, uncomfortable reciprocation or uncertain delivery may create more work than joy. When you are unsure, ask politely or choose a flexible alternative.

One thoughtful gifting example

For an interested family, a compatible smart light strip with a preset warm-white scene could be a practical festive gift.

Add a brief message about the reason you chose it. That personal connection is often what makes the present memorable long after Diwali.

Three practical moves from the original slide

Preserved from slide 22 of the supplied presentation. These are the slide's general guiding points; the article above adapts them to this specific topic.

  1. Start with the recipient’s routineMatch the gift to what they own, their space, preferences, and accessibility needs.
  2. Compare total cost and compatibilityCheck warranty, voltage, subscriptions, sizing, compatibility, and return windows.
  3. Prioritize safe and simple useReview ingredients, certifications, age guidance, and small-parts warnings.

Final thought

The right present does not need to be the most expensive or fashionable one. Take the ideas above as a starting point, respect the recipient's priorities and make the greeting genuine.
