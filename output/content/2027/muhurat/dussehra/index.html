Dussehra 2027 in short

In short

Dussehra 2027 is on Saturday 9 October. Dashami runs from about 9:02 AM IST on 9 October to 11:40 AM IST on 10 October, covering the afternoon of the 9th. In New Delhi the Vijay muhurat is 2:05 PM – 2:52 PM, the Aparahna puja 1:18 PM – 3:38 PM and Ravan Dahan follows sunset. Mumbai's Vijay muhurat is 2:24 PM – 3:11 PM, London's 2:39 PM – 3:23 PM and New York's 2:37 PM – 3:23 PM; Australia and New Zealand keep Sunday 10 October.

What is done on Dussehra, and when

  • Afternoon

    Aparajita and Shami puja

    In the Aparahna (1:18 PM – 3:38 PM in New Delhi), the goddess Aparajita is worshipped and the Shami tree honoured; Shami leaves are exchanged as 'gold' in Maharashtra and Karnataka.

  • Most auspicious

    Vijay muhurat

    The eleventh daytime muhurta (2:05 PM – 2:52 PM in New Delhi) is held to bring victory to anything begun in it: journeys, studies, business.

  • Tools of work

    Shastra and Ayudha puja

    Weapons, tools, vehicles and books are cleaned and worshipped, continuing the Ayudha Puja of Navami.

  • Evening

    Ravan Dahan

    After sunset (5:59 PM in New Delhi), effigies of Ravana, Meghnad and Kumbhakarna are burnt at Ramlila grounds.

  • East

    Durga visarjan

    In Bengal and the east, Bijoya Dashami closes Durga Puja with immersion and sindoor khela; Bengali panjikas fix the timing.

  • South

    Vidyarambham

    In Kerala and Karnataka, small children write their first letters in rice on Vijayadashami.

Where Dussehra is celebrated

Mysuru Dasara ends with the Jamboo Savari elephant procession; Kullu Dussehra in Himachal begins on Vijayadashami and runs for a week; Delhi's Ramlila grounds and Varanasi's Ramnagar close the Ramlila with Ravan Dahan; and in Nepal, Dashain continues with tika and jamara from the elders.

How the muhurat is calculated

  1. DashamiFrom about 9:02 AM IST on 9 October to 11:40 AM IST on 10 October 2027 (ephemeris adjusted to Drik Panchang), converted to local time.
  2. The dayThe day on which Dashami covers the most of Aparahna, the fourth fifth of daylight.
  3. Vijay muhuratThe eleventh of the fifteen daytime muhurtas, inside Dashami.
  4. Ravan DahanLocal sunset; effigies are burnt after it.

The 2026 version of this method matched Kolkata's published Vijay muhurat within a minute. Navratri 2027 dates (Durga Ashtami 7 October, Navami 8 October, Dussehra 9 October) agree with published calendars.

Dussehra 2027: questions people ask

When is Dussehra in 2027?

Saturday 9 October 2027, the tenth day after Navratri begins on 30 September. Dashami covers the afternoon of the 9th in India, Europe and the Americas; Australia and New Zealand keep Sunday 10 October.

What is the Vijay muhurat on Dussehra 2027?

The eleventh daytime muhurta inside Dashami: 2:05 PM – 2:52 PM in New Delhi, 2:24 PM – 3:11 PM in Mumbai, 1:22 PM – 2:09 PM in Kolkata and 2:37 PM – 3:23 PM in New York.

What time is Ravan Dahan in 2027?

After sunset on 9 October: sunset is 5:59 PM in New Delhi. Ramlila committees fix the exact time, usually soon after dark.

What is the Aparahna puja?

The fourth fifth of daylight, when Aparajita and Shami puja are done: 1:18 PM – 3:38 PM in New Delhi.

Is Dussehra one of the most auspicious days of the year?

Yes. Vijayadashami is one of the three and a half 'sade teen' muhurats when any new venture can be started without consulting a separate muhurat.

How accurate are these timings?

Dashami's timing comes from an ephemeris adjusted to Drik Panchang, typically within a minute; the 2026 method matched Kolkata's published Vijay muhurat within a minute.
