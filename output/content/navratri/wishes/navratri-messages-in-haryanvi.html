थारा or तेरा? Haryanvi has a respectful and a casual "you". थम, थमनै, थारा are respectful, right for tau-tai, parents, in-laws and teachers; 24 of these wishes use them. तू, तेरा, तेरी are casual, for a yaar or a younger cousin, as in wishes 24 and 25. To make any wish casual, swap थारे for तेरे: माता रानी की किरपा तेरे घर पै सदा बणी रहवै।

How Haryana celebrates Navratri

In Haryana, the nine nights are the naurate. On the first morning a kalash is set up and खेतरी, barley, is sown beside it in a clay pot to sprout over the nine days. In many villages girls make सांझी on the wall of the house and sing to her every evening. Families fast, keep a jot burning, hold jagratas, and on Ashtami or Navami wash the feet of the kanjaks, the young girls of the neighbourhood, and feed them halwa, puri and chana. The festival ends with Dussehra, when Sanjhi is taken to the pond and Ravan burns on the village ground.

Quick answers

Haryanvi Navratri wishes: questions people ask

How do you say Happy Navratri in Haryanvi?

Say "नौरत्यां की घणी-घणी बधाई" (Nauratyan ki ghani-ghani badhai, many many Navratri greetings), "शुभ नौरते" (Shubh Naurate) or, the Haryana way, "नौरत्यां की राम-राम" (Ram-Ram for Navratri).

What is the difference between थारा and तेरा in Haryanvi?

थारा (thara) goes with थम/तम, the respectful you, and is right for elders and for wishing a family. तेरा (tera) goes with तू and is casual, for close friends and younger cousins. Wishes 24 and 25 on this page use the casual form; the rest are respectful.

What is Sanjhi in Haryana?

Sanjhi is a Navratri folk tradition of Haryana. Girls and women make an image of the goddess on the wall of the house with clay and dung, decorate it with stars, the sun and the moon, sing Sanjhi songs each evening, and immerse it on Dussehra.

What is khetri?

Khetri is the barley sown in a clay pot or a patch of soil on the first day of Navratri, next to the kalash. It sprouts over the nine days, and the green shoots are taken as a blessing at the end of the festival.

Where is Haryanvi spoken?

Haryanvi, also called Bangru, is spoken across Haryana and in the villages around Delhi, with related speech in neighbouring parts of western Uttar Pradesh. It is written in Devanagari, and spellings vary from district to district.

When is Navratri 2026?

Sharad Navratri 2026 begins with Ghatasthapana on Sunday 11 October; the muhurat in New Delhi is 6:19 to 10:12 AM. Durga Ashtami and Maha Navami fall on Monday 19 October in India, and Dussehra (Vijayadashami) is on Tuesday 20 October.
