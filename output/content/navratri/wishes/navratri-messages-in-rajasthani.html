Rajasthani Navratri wishes in short

The quickest way to say Happy Navratri in Rajasthani is नवरात्रा री घणी-घणी बधायां (Navratra ri ghani-ghani badhayan). For elders, add the respectful Khamma Ghani or Ram-Ram sa. Below are 26 Rajasthani wishes with English meanings, a guide to which to send whom, and the Rajasthani words they use. Navratri 2026 runs from Sunday 11 October to Dussehra on Tuesday 20 October.

आप or थारे? Rajasthani, like Hindi, has a respectful and a familiar "you". Wishes with आप, आपरे, आपणे and a closing सा are right for parents, in-laws, teachers and elders. Wishes with थारे, थारो, थारी are affectionate and close, for friends, siblings and the children of the family.

How Rajasthan celebrates Navratri

In Rajasthan, Navratri begins as it does across north India, with Ghatasthapana: a kalash is set up, जवारा (barley) is sown in a clay bowl and an akhand jyot is lit for the nine days. Families visit the temple of their kuldevi, keep fasts, sing bhajans of Mata Rani at night jagrans and feed young girls on Ashtami or Navami. Greetings are exchanged in the family's own tongue, which is why a wish in Rajasthani lands so much warmer than one in standard Hindi.

अ
Amer, Jaipur

Shila Devi Temple

The goddess of the Kachhwaha rulers, inside Amer Fort. The temple is at its busiest during the Navratri fair, when devotees climb up to the fort for darshan.

क
Deshnoke, Bikaner

Karni Mata Temple

The temple of Karni Mata, a form of Durga, known for the thousands of kabas (rats) that live in it. Fairs are held during both Navratris each year.

को
Kota, Hadoti

Kota Dussehra Mela

Navratri leads into Kota's Dussehra fair, one of the largest in India, with tall Ravana effigies, a long fair and cultural evenings.

ग
Udaipur, Banswara, Dungarpur

Garba in Mewar and Vagad

Southern Rajasthan shares its border and its music with Gujarat, so neighbourhood Garba and Dandiya nights are a big part of Navratri here.

Quick answers

Rajasthani Navratri wishes: questions people ask

How do you say Happy Navratri in Rajasthani?

The most common forms are "नवरात्रा री घणी-घणी बधायां" (Navratra ri ghani-ghani badhayan, many many Navratri greetings), "शुभ नवरात्रा" (Shubh Navratra) and "नवरात्रा री राम-राम सा" (Navratra ri Ram-Ram sa). For elders, add Khamma Ghani: "नवरात्रा री खम्मा घणी सा".

What does Khamma Ghani mean?

Khamma Ghani is Rajasthan's respectful greeting, used when meeting elders, guests and people you want to honour. It is often explained as wishing someone much well-being, and it is answered with "Khamma Ghani sa" or "Ghani Khamma". In these wishes it works as "warm, respectful greetings".

Is Rajasthani the same as Marwari?

Marwari is the most widely spoken variety of Rajasthani. Other varieties include Mewari around Udaipur, Dhundhari around Jaipur, Hadoti around Kota and Shekhawati. These 26 wishes use common Marwari-leaning forms such as थारो, री and घणी that are understood across the state; spellings vary from region to region.

Which wishes should I send to elders?

Choose the wishes that use the respectful आप, आपरे or आपणे and end with "sa": numbers 1, 2, 3, 5, 11, 16, 19 and 26. Wishes with थारे or थारो are warm and familiar, better for friends, siblings and younger relatives.

When is Navratri 2026?

Sharad Navratri 2026 begins with Ghatasthapana on Sunday 11 October; the muhurat in New Delhi is 6:19 to 10:12 AM. Durga Ashtami and Maha Navami fall on Monday 19 October in India, and Dussehra (Vijayadashami) is on Tuesday 20 October.

How do I send these wishes on WhatsApp?

Tap WhatsApp under any wish. It opens WhatsApp with the wish and a link to it already typed, so you only have to pick the chat. Copy puts the wish and its link on your clipboard for any other app, and on a phone, Share opens your phone's share sheet.
