Punjabi Navratri wishes in short

The Punjabi way to say Happy Navratri is ਨਰਾਤਿਆਂ ਦੀਆਂ ਲੱਖ-ਲੱਖ ਵਧਾਈਆਂ (Naratiyan diyan lakh-lakh vadhaiyan), or simply ਸ਼ੁਭ ਨਰਾਤੇ (Shubh Narate). Below are 26 Punjabi wishes in Gurmukhi, each with Roman script and English meaning, a guide to which to send whom, and the Punjabi words they use. Navratri 2026 runs from Sunday 11 October to Dussehra on Tuesday 20 October.

ਤੁਸੀਂ or ਤੂੰ? Punjabi has a respectful and a casual "you". ਤੁਸੀਂ, ਤੁਹਾਡਾ, ਤੁਹਾਨੂੰ are respectful, right for Biji, Bhapa ji, in-laws and teachers; 24 of these wishes use them. ਤੂੰ, ਤੇਰਾ, ਤੇਰੀ are casual, for a yaar or a younger cousin, as in wishes 24 and 25. To make a wish casual, swap ਤੁਹਾਡੇ for ਤੇਰੇ: ਮਾਤਾ ਰਾਣੀ ਦੀ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਤੇਰੇ ਘਰ ਉੱਤੇ ਸਦਾ ਬਣੀ ਰਹੇ।

How Punjab celebrates Navratri

In Punjabi homes, the narate begin with a jot lit before Mata Rani and ਖੇਤਰੀ, barley, sown in a clay pot to sprout over the nine days. Many families fast, hold a Mata di chaunki or an all-night jagrata where bhetan are sung and jaikaras called out, and visit the goddess temples of their city. On Ashtami or Navami the kanjaks, the young girls of the neighbourhood, are welcomed, their feet washed, a red thread tied on their wrists, and they are fed halwa, puri and kale chhole. The nine days end with Dussehra and the Ravan Dahan grounds of every town.

Amritsar

Durgiana Mandir and the Langur Mela

During Navratri, children dressed as langurs, Hanuman's army, come to the Bada Hanuman Mandir at Durgiana to fulfil family vows. The mela ends on Dussehra.

Patiala

Sri Kali Devi Mandir

The Kali Devi temple facing the Baradari gardens, built under Maharaja Bhupinder Singh, is the city's busiest shrine through the nine days.

ਤ
Jalandhar

Sri Devi Talab Mandir

Revered as a Shakti Peetha, with its temple beside a sacred tank, Devi Talab draws devotees from across Doaba during Navratri.

ਮ
The hill shrines

Chintpurni, Jwala Ji, Naina Devi

Many Punjabi families make a yatra to the goddess shrines in the Himachal hills, and to Vaishno Devi, during or right after Navratri.

Quick answers

Punjabi Navratri wishes: questions people ask

How do you say Happy Navratri in Punjabi?

Say "ਨਰਾਤਿਆਂ ਦੀਆਂ ਲੱਖ-ਲੱਖ ਵਧਾਈਆਂ" (Naratiyan diyan lakh-lakh vadhaiyan, a hundred thousand Navratri congratulations), "ਸ਼ੁਭ ਨਰਾਤੇ" (Shubh Narate) or "ਨਰਾਤੇ ਮੁਬਾਰਕ" (Narate mubarak).

Why do Punjabis say Narate instead of Navratri?

ਨਰਾਤੇ (Narate) is the everyday Punjabi form of Navratri, used in homes across Punjab, Delhi and the diaspora. The full form ਨਵਰਾਤਰੀ is also correct, and you will see both in wishes.

What is the difference between ਤੁਸੀਂ and ਤੂੰ?

ਤੁਸੀਂ (tusi) and ਤੁਹਾਡਾ (tuhada) are the respectful you and your, right for elders, in-laws and teachers. ਤੂੰ (tu) and ਤੇਰਾ (tera) are casual, for close friends and younger cousins. Wishes 24 and 25 use the casual form; the rest are respectful.

Can I send these if my family doesn't read Gurmukhi?

Yes. Each wish has a Roman transliteration under the Gurmukhi, so you can read it aloud or send the Roman line instead. Punjabi is also written in Shahmukhi script in Pakistan.

What is the Langur Mela in Amritsar?

During Navratri, families whose wish for a child was fulfilled dress their children as langurs, Hanuman's army, and bring them to the Bada Hanuman Mandir at Durgiana in Amritsar. The children keep the vow through the nine days, and the mela ends on Dussehra.

When is Navratri 2026?

Sharad Navratri 2026 begins with Ghatasthapana on Sunday 11 October; the muhurat in New Delhi is 6:19 to 10:12 AM. Durga Ashtami and Maha Navami fall on Monday 19 October in India, and Dussehra (Vijayadashami) is on Tuesday 20 October.
