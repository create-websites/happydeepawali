உங்கள் or உன்? Tamil has a respectful and a familiar "you". நீங்கள், உங்கள், உங்களுக்கு are respectful, right for elders, in-laws and teachers; 24 of these wishes use them. நீ, உன், உனக்கு are familiar, for close friends and younger family, as in wishes 23 and 24. To make a wish familiar, swap உங்கள் for உன்: அம்மன் அருளால் உன் இல்லம் என்றும் மங்களமாக இருக்கட்டும்.

How Tamil Nadu celebrates Navarathri

In Tamil homes Navarathri means golu. On or just before the first day, the dolls come out of storage and are arranged on steps, with the kalasam and the gods at the top. The nine nights are shared among the three goddesses: the first three for Durga, the next three for Lakshmi and the last three for Saraswati. Every evening neighbours drop in, songs are sung before the golu, sundal is served, and women guests leave with thamboolam. On Navami books and instruments are placed before Saraswati and tools before the Goddess for Ayudha Pooja, and on Vijayadasami children write their first letters and one doll is laid down to mark the end of the golu.

Tamil Navarathri wishes: questions people ask

How do you say Happy Navarathri in Tamil?

Say "இனிய நவராத்திரி நல்வாழ்த்துகள்" (Iniya Navarathri nalvazhthukkal), happy Navarathri wishes. For a short greeting, "இனிய நவராத்திரி!" (Iniya Navarathri!) is enough.

What is golu?

Golu (கொலு, bommai kolu) is the Navarathri display of dolls on steps, usually an odd number such as five, seven or nine, with gods and goddesses at the top and scenes of everyday life lower down. Families invite friends to see it each evening, sing, serve sundal and give thamboolam to women guests.

How do I invite someone to my golu in Tamil?

Say "எங்கள் வீட்டுக் கொலுவுக்கு உங்களை அன்புடன் அழைக்கிறோம்" (Engal veettu koluvukku ungalai anbudan azhaikkirom), we lovingly invite you to the golu at our home. Add the date, time and address. Wish 8 on this page is a ready-made invitation.

What are Saraswathi Pooja and Ayudha Pooja?

On Navami, the ninth day, Tamil homes worship Saraswati by placing books, notebooks and musical instruments before her, and keep Ayudha Pooja by cleaning and worshipping tools, machines and vehicles. The next day, Vijayadasami, the books are taken back for study and children begin their first letters at Vidyarambham.

What is the difference between உங்கள் and உன்?

உங்கள் (ungal) and நீங்கள் (neengal) are the respectful your and you, for elders, in-laws, teachers and anyone you are not close to. உன் (un) and நீ (nee) are familiar, for close friends and younger family. Wishes 23 and 24 use the familiar form; the rest are respectful.

When is Navarathri 2026?

Sharad Navarathri 2026 begins on Sunday 11 October; the Ghatasthapana muhurat in New Delhi is 6:19 to 10:12 AM. Navami, with Saraswathi Pooja and Ayudha Pooja, falls on Monday 19 October in India, and Vijayadasami on Tuesday 20 October. Check your local panchangam for your city.

Tap WhatsApp under any wish. It opens WhatsApp with the Tamil text and a link to it already typed, so you only have to pick the chat. Copy Roman copies the English-letter version instead, and on a phone, Share opens your phone's share sheet.
