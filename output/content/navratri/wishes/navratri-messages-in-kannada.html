Kannada Navaratri wishes in short

To say Happy Navaratri in Kannada, write ನವರಾತ್ರಿ ಹಬ್ಬದ ಹಾರ್ದಿಕ ಶುಭಾಶಯಗಳು (Navaratri habbada hardika shubhashayagalu); for Mysuru Dasara, ನಾಡಹಬ್ಬ ದಸರಾದ ಶುಭಾಶಯಗಳು. Below are 26 Kannada wishes, each with an English-letter version and meaning: for the family, for Bombe Habba, for Chamundeshwari, Lakshmi and Sharade, and for Ayudha Puje and Vijayadashami. Navaratri 2026 runs from Sunday 11 October to Vijayadashami on Tuesday 20 October.

ನಿಮ್ಮ or ನಿನ್ನ? Kannada has a respectful and a familiar "you". ನೀವು, ನಿಮ್ಮ, ನಿಮಗೆ are respectful, right for elders, in-laws and teachers; 24 of these wishes use them. ನೀನು, ನಿನ್ನ, ನಿನಗೆ are familiar, for close friends and younger family, as in wishes 23 and 24. To make a wish familiar, swap ನಿಮ್ಮ for ನಿನ್ನ: ಮಹಾಲಕ್ಷ್ಮಿ ನಿನ್ನ ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಸಿರಿ ಸಂಪತ್ತು ತುಂಬಲಿ.

Kannada words in these wishes, explained

Know what you are sending. These are the Kannada words that make the wishes sound like home, with how to say them.

KannadaSay itMeaning

ನೀನು · ನಿನ್ನ · ನಿನಗೆneenu · ninna · ninageYou · your · to you, familiar, for close friends and younger family. Wishes 23 and 24 use it.

ನಾಡಹಬ್ಬNadahabba"Festival of the land": Mysuru Dasara, Karnataka's state festival.

ತಾಯಿ · ಮಾತೆtayi · mateMother; how the Goddess is addressed.

ಪಟ್ಟದ ಗೊಂಬೆpattada gombeThe pair of bride and groom dolls at the heart of a Karnataka doll display, often handed down from mother to daughter.

ವಿಜಯದಶಮಿVijayadashamiThe tenth day of victory, the day of the Jamboo Savari.

How Karnataka celebrates Navaratri and Dasara

In Karnataka, Navaratri is Dasara, and Mysuru Dasara is the state's Nada Habba, the festival of the land. At home, families set up the bombe, dolls arranged on steps around the pattada gombe, the bride and groom pair, and neighbours come each evening to see them and leave with arishina-kumkuma. In the last days, books are placed before Sharade for Saraswati Puje, and on Mahanavami tools, machines and vehicles are washed and worshipped for Ayudha Puje. On Vijayadashami the Jamboo Savari winds through Mysuru, children begin their first letters, and people exchange banni leaves as gold and touch their elders' feet.

Mysuru Palace and the Jamboo Savari

Karnataka's Nada Habba: the palace is lit every evening of Dasara, and on Vijayadashami the Goddess Chamundeshwari is carried through the city in a golden howdah on an elephant.

Chamundeshwari Temple

The temple of the goddess who slew Mahishasura, above the city. Mysuru Dasara is inaugurated here, and devotees climb the thousand steps through Navaratri.

Sharada Peetham

Sharadamba, Saraswati, is dressed in a different alankara each day of Sharannavaratri at the Sringeri math, with special worship through the nine days.

Mangaluru Dasara and huli vesha

On the coast, Mangaluru Dasara ends with a procession of the goddess Sharada from Kudroli, while troupes of huli vesha tiger dancers fill the streets of Udupi and Mangaluru.

Quick answers

Kannada Navaratri wishes: questions people ask

How do you say Happy Navaratri in Kannada?

Say "ನವರಾತ್ರಿ ಹಬ್ಬದ ಹಾರ್ದಿಕ ಶುಭಾಶಯಗಳು" (Navaratri habbada hardika shubhashayagalu), heartfelt Navaratri festival wishes, or simply "ಶುಭ ನವರಾತ್ರಿ" (Shubha Navaratri). For Dasara, "ನಾಡಹಬ್ಬ ದಸರಾದ ಶುಭಾಶಯಗಳು".

What is Nada Habba Dasara?

Nada Habba means "festival of the land". Mysuru Dasara is Karnataka's state festival: it is inaugurated at the Chamundeshwari Temple on Chamundi Hills, Mysuru Palace is lit every evening, and on Vijayadashami the Jamboo Savari procession carries the Goddess through the city on an elephant.

What is Bombe Habba?

Bombe Habba, the doll festival, is Karnataka's Navaratri display of dolls on steps, like golu in Tamil Nadu. At its heart is the pattada gombe, a pair of bride and groom dolls often passed from mother to daughter. Neighbours are invited to see the dolls, and women guests are given arishina-kumkuma.

Why do people exchange banni leaves on Vijayadashami?

On Vijayadashami, people in Karnataka worship the banni (shami) tree and give its leaves to elders and friends as "gold", saying "ಬನ್ನಿ ತಗೊಂಡು ಬಂಗಾರದ ಹಾಗೆ ಇರೋಣ": let's take banni and stay like gold. Younger people touch elders' feet as they give the leaves. Wish 15 on this page uses the saying.

What is the difference between ನಿಮ್ಮ and ನಿನ್ನ?

ನೀವು (neevu) and ನಿಮ್ಮ (nimma) are the respectful you and your, for elders, in-laws, teachers and anyone you are not close to. ನೀನು (neenu) and ನಿನ್ನ (ninna) are familiar, for close friends and younger family. Wishes 23 and 24 use the familiar form; the rest are respectful.

When is Navaratri 2026?

Sharad Navaratri 2026 begins on Sunday 11 October; the Ghatasthapana muhurat in New Delhi is 6:19 to 10:12 AM. Mahanavami, with Ayudha Puje, falls on Monday 19 October in India, and Vijayadashami, with the Jamboo Savari, on Tuesday 20 October. Check your local panchanga for your city.
