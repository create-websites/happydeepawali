Giving Back Locally During Thanksgiving

To support food banks, shelters, and delivery programs during Thanksgiving, consider asking organizations what they specifically need and find ways to provide year-round assistance while still maintaining holiday spirit.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🍁 ThanksgivingGratitude, Wellness & Traditions

Ask organizations what is actually useful

Before planning a donation drive, be sure to check the specific needs, storage constraints, and volunteer guidelines of food banks and shelters. It's worth looking at the organization's wish list to ensure that your donation will have the greatest impact. Cash donations can often stretch further than retail purchases, as organizations can buy food in bulk for a better value.

Think beyond canned-food drives

Different communities may have varying needs, such as hygiene products, transportation aid, meal delivery, winter attire, gift cards, translation services, or ongoing financial assistance, all of which can be valuable forms of support.

Volunteer with humility

Holiday volunteer opportunities may be limited, so if Thanksgiving Day is already full, consider volunteering on the days before or after, or commit to a regular shift later in the winter. Please follow the instructions of staff members and refrain from taking photos of recipients without their explicit permission.

Make service part of family tradition

Children can assist in organizing pantry-safe donations, crafting supportive cards when requested by organizations, or collectively selecting a cause as a family. The aim is to instill routines of community involvement beyond viewing a single holiday as the sole occasion for providing assistance.

Frequently asked questions

Is donating money better than donating food?

Food banks can efficiently stretch cash, but it's important to check their guidance before making a decision due to varying needs.

What if Thanksgiving volunteer shifts are full?

Volunteer for an additional day or provide ongoing support. Organizations require assistance year-round, not just during holidays.
