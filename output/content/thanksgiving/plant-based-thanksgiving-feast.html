A Complete Plant-Based Thanksgiving Feast

Create a delicious vegetarian or vegan Thanksgiving spread featuring main dishes, side dishes, gravies, and desserts that are both satisfying and festive, not just an afterthought.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🍁 ThanksgivingCulinary Guides & Recipes

Design around a real centerpiece

For a successful plant-based Thanksgiving, it's important to have a main dish that is hearty, fragrant, and visually appealing. Instead of relying solely on side dishes, consider options like a mushroom and lentil Wellington, stuffed squash, savory nut roast, vegetable pot pie, or layered filo pie.

Build depth with umami and browning

Use mushrooms, caramelized onions, miso, toasted nuts, roasted garlic, and deeply browned vegetables to infuse your dishes with the rich, savory flavors typically found in slow-cooked meat dishes. Incorporate them into gravies, stuffings, and main courses for a cohesive and flavorful meal experience.

Classic sides are easy to adapt

Mashed potatoes can be made with olive oil, plant butter, and unsweetened plant milk. Stuffing can be prepared with vegetable stock. Green bean casserole can be made with dairy-free mushroom sauce. Many cranberry sauce recipes are naturally plant-based.

Be sure to carefully check packaged ingredients as some stocks, pie crusts, marshmallows, and flavorings may contain animal-derived ingredients.

Dessert should still feel like Thanksgiving

You can use plant-based custard to make pumpkin pie, and apple crisp, fruit galettes, and pecan-style tarts are also versatile options. To end the meal on a special note, offer coffee, tea, and a nonalcoholic sparkling drink for a festive touch.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good vegan centerpiece?

Stuffed squash, mushroom-lentil Wellington, vegetable pot pie, and savory nut roasts are all hearty main dishes that make a statement.

Do I need separate dishes for non-vegan guests?

A plant-based menu that is well-designed can cater to all individuals. If you choose to include dairy or meat options, it is recommended to keep them separate from each dish instead of integrating them into every meal.
