Why Football Became a Thanksgiving Tradition

Trace the evolution of Thanksgiving football from college games in the late 1800s to its inclusion in the NFL schedule in Detroit and Dallas, becoming a staple in many holiday traditions.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🍁 ThanksgivingHistory, Heritage & Perspectives

College football helped set the pattern

In the late 19th century, college football games frequently coincided with Thanksgiving, making it a popular choice for rivalry matchups due to the convenience of students and spectators having time off from their usual schedules, long before the NFL as we know it today.

The match was a natural fit: as both football and Thanksgiving grew in popularity as public traditions, newspaper coverage played a key role in transforming significant games into community-wide celebrations.

The NFL builds an annual tradition

The tradition of professional football on Thanksgiving dates back to 1920, with Detroit hosting its first game in 1934 and the Lions playing annually since 1945. Dallas became a regular Thanksgiving host in 1966, further solidifying the holiday's association with NFL games.

In 2006, a Thanksgiving night game was introduced, extending the schedule beyond the traditional Detroit and Dallas games. This has turned into a full-day media event for numerous households.

Why the tradition stuck

Thanksgiving football is the perfect addition to the day's festivities, providing a shared background for cooking, visiting, and relaxing after the meal. It offers a simple communal activity for multigenerational gatherings, accommodating guests with varying interests.

Not all households follow the same tradition on Thanksgiving. Some may choose to skip sports altogether, while others may plan their entire afternoon around kickoff. Thanksgiving can cater to both preferences.

Make football serve the gathering: not dominate it

To accommodate different guest preferences, consider creating separate areas for watching games, socializing, and dining during gatherings. By maintaining a moderate volume and avoiding a television as the focal point of the dining table, you can help ensure that everyone feels included in the tradition.

Frequently asked questions

When did NFL Thanksgiving games begin?

In its inaugural season in 1920, the NFL began keeping track of Thanksgiving games.

Why are the Lions and Cowboys associated with Thanksgiving?

Detroit started hosting Thanksgiving games regularly in the 1930s and has done so every year since 1945, while Dallas began its own tradition in 1966.
