Thanksgiving Leftovers: Gourmet Transformations

Transform your leftover turkey, stuffing, cranberry sauce, vegetables, and pie into delicious soups, sandwiches, casseroles, and new meals by adhering to proper storage guidelines.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🍁 ThanksgivingCulinary Guides & Recipes

Cool and store leftovers first

To kickstart your creative process, follow USDA guidelines by refrigerating any perishable leftovers within two hours of cooking or serving. For large portions, use shallow containers to help them cool down faster.

It is recommended to use refrigerated Thanksgiving leftovers within four days, and any excess should be frozen.

Turkey becomes a new protein

Employ sliced turkey in a pressed sandwich with cranberry sauce and a tangy condiment, incorporate diced turkey into enchiladas, mix it into pot pie filling, or briefly simmer it in a broth-based soup. To prevent overcooking, only reheat the necessary portion.

Stuffing, potatoes and vegetables

Stuffing can transform into crispy patties that can be topped with an egg, a savory waffle, or used as the base for a casserole. Mashed potatoes have the potential to turn into potato cakes or act as a thickening agent for soup. Roasted vegetables are versatile and can be added to grain bowls, frittatas, or blended soups with great

Desserts can become components

Break up pie to make parfaits, transform pumpkin filling into a milkshake or yogurt swirl, or freeze for future use. By planning leftovers, you can avoid waste and keep your post-Thanksgiving meals exciting and fresh.

Note: Store perishable foods in the refrigerator right away; if unsure about how to store them, it's better to throw them out than risk eating

Frequently asked questions

How long are Thanksgiving leftovers safe in the refrigerator?

According to USDA recommendations, refrigerated Thanksgiving leftovers can be safely enjoyed for up to four days if they are promptly cooled and stored properly.

Can I freeze Thanksgiving leftovers?

Yes, divide into meal-sized portions, date label, and store in freezer-safe containers for optimal quality.
