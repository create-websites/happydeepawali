Autumn Thanksgiving Tablescapes on a Budget

Design a chic Thanksgiving table using gourds, foliage, pinecones, candles, layered linens, name cards, and sustainable decor in place of costly centerpieces.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🍁 ThanksgivingHosting, Prep & Decor

Start with texture, not shopping

You don't need a big budget to create a memorable table setting. Start with basic items like neutral plates, simple glassware, fabric napkins, and a runner. Enhance the look with autumnal touches such as leaves, pinecones, branches, small pumpkins, or seasonal fruit.

Keep the centerpiece low

To ensure guests can see each other, opt for small clusters of gourds, bud vases, low greenery, or candles instead of a single tall arrangement. When using candles, always keep flames at a safe distance from dry leaves, paper, and children.

Use napkins and place cards as color

Using a folded cloth napkin, ribbon, or handwritten name card can create a stronger visual impact than adding extra objects. By incorporating two or three colors throughout the table, even inexpensive items can feel purposeful.

Design for food service

Decide on the placement of serving dishes before arranging the table. For family-style dinners, ensure there are ample open spaces. For buffet-style dinners, focus on making the dining table more decorative as the platters will be placed elsewhere.

Frequently asked questions

What is the easiest inexpensive centerpiece?

A lineup of petite pumpkins or gourds intertwined with trimmed greenery and a handful of small candles is uncomplicated, eco-friendly, and adjustable in size.

Should I decorate with real leaves?

You can use artificial or fresh greenery indoors instead of dry leaves near open flames and food surfaces.
