The Evolution of the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade

Follow the evolution of the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade from a small employee march in 1924 to the extravagant display of giant balloons, performances, and a beloved television event.

Read the guideAll 20 articles
🍁 ThanksgivingHistory, Heritage & Perspectives

A holiday parade begins in 1924

The Macy's Thanksgiving parade originated in 1924, as employees dressed in costumes paraded through Manhattan alongside animals from Central Park Zoo en route to the Herald Square store. The event was created to provide festive entertainment and kick off the holiday shopping season.

The parade soon became a yearly event, evolving its visual style alongside the city. Live animals were replaced by elaborate displays like floats, marching bands, celebrity appearances, and the iconic giant character balloons.

How the balloons became icons

The parade was instantly recognizable from a distance and on TV screens across the country, thanks to the oversized balloons. The art of balloon design evolved into a complex process involving illustration, fabrication, testing, logistics, and weather considerations.

The balloons added a modern touch to the parade, making it relevant to current pop culture trends. While characters may change over time, the tradition of watching giant figures glide through the streets of Manhattan stayed constant.

Broadway, television and national reach

As broadcasting grew, the parade evolved from a New York street event into a spectacle that included musical performances, marching bands, dance groups, and theatrical segments for both viewers at home and spectators along the route.

The parade's longevity can be attributed to its dual nature: a physical civic event and a televised production. Families can witness it in person, from their living rooms, or through online clips, but the central concept remains a shared holiday spectacle.

Planning a parade-centered Thanksgiving morning

When incorporating the parade into your family tradition, plan breakfast and food preparation around the broadcast instead of attempting to cook everything at once. Keep children's activities uncomplicated, and be aware that the televised schedule and public viewing information may vary annually: consult official sources closer to the holiday.

Frequently asked questions

When did the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade begin?

Macy’s dates the first parade to 1924.

Were giant balloons part of the very first parade?

Early parades featured live animals, while giant character balloons became a prominent aspect in later years.
